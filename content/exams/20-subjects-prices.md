**This is the one place for online exam prices.** The format of each subject is fixed by Pearson: the learner can't choose paper-based or onscreen.

- **Paper-based:** questions appear on screen; the learner writes on a printed answer booklet, then scans and uploads it straight after.
- **Onscreen:** questions and typed answers are both on screen.
