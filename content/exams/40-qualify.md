## Qualify the customer before they book

Run through these on the call. If any answer is no, talk it through first. **If they can't meet it, the centre exam is the right product.**

1. The subject is on the [list](#/kb/p?slug=exams-prices), and a Pearson Edexcel International qualification works for their college, university or employer (they should check their course).
2. Valid, in-date photo ID: passport, national ID card, full or provisional driving licence, or UK citizenship card. **Expired ID is refused.**
3. A laptop or desktop (not a tablet) with webcam, microphone and speakers, running Windows, macOS or ChromeOS, with Google Chrome. A personal device, not a school or work one.
4. A phone or tablet with a working camera (Android 8 or iOS 15.8 or later) for Pearson's app.
5. A separate backup device to record the exam.
6. For paper-based subjects: a printer with enough ink and paper (up to 40 pages per exam) and a scanner or scanning app.
7. Internet with at least 5 Mbps upload (10 Mbps recommended).
8. A quiet, private room. A shared or communal space is not allowed.
9. **An adult over 18 at home for every exam**, whatever the learner's age, who isn't their tutor or teacher.
10. They can do the familiarisation between 23 Feb and 3 Mar 2027.
11. If abroad: remote invigilation runs in their country, and they're fine with UK exam times.
12. If they need access arrangements: they're on the allowed list below, and they book by 13 Dec with evidence.

## What they need at home: four devices

Most people are surprised by the kit list, so set expectations early.

| Device | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Laptop or desktop | Shows the exam. Webcam, microphone, speakers, Chrome, screen sharing, plugged in to power, one screen only |
| 2. Phone or tablet | Runs Pearson's ProctorExam app and films the room during the exam. Fully charged, on silent |
| 3. Backup recorder | Records the whole exam about 3 metres away in case the internet drops. Needs no internet. Keep the recording until results day |
| 4. The adult's phone | For the responsible adult to call Pearson's helpline in an emergency. Not the phone used in the exam |

Plus, for paper-based subjects: a printer, ink and paper (print at 100% size), a scanning app with no page limit, and an A4 envelope to keep each finished paper.

## Access arrangements

**Available online, with evidence**, booked by **13 Dec 2026** for Summer 2027:

- 25% extra time
- Supervised rest breaks
- Fidget toys
- Ear defenders

**Not available online:** readers, scribes, prompters, 50% extra time or other personalised arrangements. **Sell the centre exam instead.**

- Pearson can arrange a female invigilator on request.
- Religious or cultural requests need the centre to write to Pearson at least 4 weeks before the exams: pass them to the exams team.

Access arrangements are **for calls only**. They don't go on web pages, ads or blogs.
