## After 25 Jan 2027

Sell the next series:

- **International GCSE:** November 2027.
- **International A Level:** October 2027 or January 2028.

## Our cut-offs, not theirs

Our cut-offs are a week before Tutors & Exams' (theirs are 1 Feb and 18 Dec), so we have time to make the entry. **Don't quote Tutors & Exams' dates to customers.**
