## Questions customers ask

**Is an International GCSE the same as a GCSE?**
It's a different qualification from a different specification, graded 9 to 1 like GCSE. Colleges, universities and most employers accept it as equivalent. Some courses ask for a UK GCSE specifically, so the learner should check with the college, university or employer first.

**Why does it cost more than sitting at a centre?**
Pearson's remote invigilation service adds a cost: a live invigilator for every exam, plus the familiarisation. If they can get to a centre, the centre exam is cheaper and we'll book it for them.

**Do I need a reason to sit at home?**
No. Students do not need evidence or a reason to sit at home. Any student can choose remote invigilation.

**Can I sit from outside the UK?**
Yes, where Pearson offers remote invigilation in that country (it isn't available everywhere). Exams start at UK times, so check the time zone. Outside the UK, Pearson may confirm different start times. Pearson can't change start times because someone moves, so they must tell us where they'll be.

**Does the adult have to be a parent?**
No. Any responsible adult over 18 who isn't their tutor or subject teacher. It's needed for adult learners too.

**What if the internet drops during the exam?**
The exam keeps working offline. They carry on, stay in view of the backup recorder, and call Pearson's helpline when they finish. Paper-based answers are uploaded as soon as the internet is back. Pearson may ask for the backup recording and evidence from the internet provider.

**What if a scan is unreadable?**
Pearson asks for a rescan by email, up to three times. If they don't send it in time, the paper can be graded X. Late submissions aren't accepted.

**Can I use a tablet or a school laptop?**
No tablet for the exam itself. School or work laptops often block the camera, microphone or screen sharing, so a personal laptop or desktop is strongly recommended.

**What about speaking tests for French and Spanish?**
Tutors & Exams run the speaking tests online. Dates come nearer the time. Only written papers are invigilated by Pearson.

**I've used Pearson's remote service before. Do I still do familiarisation?**
Yes. It's compulsory every series.

## Refunds and when things go wrong

- **Once we've made the entry with the exam centre, no refund is due**, even inside the 14-day cooling-off period.
- **Miss the familiarisation** and Pearson removes the entry, with no refund. They'd then need a centre or a later series.
- **No refund if something on the learner's side stops the exam:** equipment, internet, ID, room or supervision.
- If Pearson's systems fail, we help them through Pearson's process, including special consideration.
- Pearson can withhold a result if its terms are broken, for example the adult isn't available or the backup recording isn't provided when asked.
