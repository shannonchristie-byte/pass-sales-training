## What happens after they book

| Step | What happens | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | They book the online product on the website (or you put the order through), choosing the series | Learner |
| 2 | Our exams team emails a details form: full name exactly as on their ID, date of birth, contact details, home postcode, the country they live in and where they'll sit, an emergency contact (name, mobile, email), their UCI if they have one, a photo of their ID, a recent photo, any access arrangement evidence, and agreement to Pearson's Remote Invigilation terms of use (a parent or guardian agrees for under-18s) | Learner, then exams team |
| 3 | We check the ID and details, make the entry with Pearson through our partner exam centre, Tutors & Exams, as a remote invigilation candidate, and confirm by email | Exams team |
| 4 | **Familiarisation (compulsory).** They get the details for Pearson's practice session and complete it at home between 23 Feb and 3 Mar 2027. It tests the software, their kit and scanning. It is not a mock and has no access arrangements | Learner |
| 5 | Sign-in details for the real exams arrive 1 to 2 weeks before the first exam. These are different from the familiarisation ones | Tutors & Exams or exams team |
| 6 | Exam day: they log in at the UK start time, show their ID to the camera, and sit with a live Pearson invigilator | Learner |
| 7 | Results and certificate come from Pearson on results day, through Tutors & Exams, and we pass them on | Exams team |

After purchase, **the exams team owns the learner**.

## The adult's role on exam day

- Present and visible to the invigilator at login.
- Leaves the room before the learner presses start, and stays nearby for the whole exam.
- Supervises any toilet break (the timer keeps running) and any loss of connection, and calls Pearson's helpline if needed.
- Comes back at the end to help with scanning and uploading.

## The rules

- **Allowed:** a clear water bottle with no label, pens, blank rough paper shown to the camera, and any materials the paper allows.
- **Not allowed:** notes, books, phones, headphones, watches, food, posters on the walls, Grammarly or similar.
- Graphical calculators must be put into exam mode on camera.
- The invigilator sees and hears the learner and replies by chat only. Learners show their ears at set-up.
- Paper-based answers are scanned into one PDF and uploaded straight after. Never send scans through social media.
