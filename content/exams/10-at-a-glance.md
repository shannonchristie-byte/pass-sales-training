Learners sit **Pearson Edexcel International GCSE** and **International A Level** exams online from home, with a live Pearson invigilator watching over the webcam. We enter them with Pearson through our partner exam centre, Tutors & Exams; they sit at home. On sale from 26 Oct 2026, starting with the Summer 2027 series.

## The pitch in 30 seconds

> "You can sit a Pearson Edexcel International GCSE or International A Level online from your own home, with a live Pearson invigilator watching over the webcam. We enter you with Pearson and send you everything you need for the ID check and Pearson's practice session, which you do at home in your own time. You don't need a reason or any evidence to sit at home."

## Selling points

- **One of the only providers in the world.** Very few providers offer this. Never say "the only" or "the first".
- **Sit from home, anywhere in the world**, wherever Pearson offers remote invigilation. Exams run on UK time.
- **Open to anyone.** Students do not need evidence or a reason to sit at home. Any student can choose remote invigilation.

## Who it suits

Adults resitting a GCSE or A Level, home-educated learners, learners with no exam centre nearby, learners living abroad, and anyone who would rather not sit in a hall.

## What it is not

UK GCSEs and A Levels (AQA, OCR, Edexcel UK, WJEC) and every Cambridge exam are still sat at a centre. Only Pearson Edexcel's International GCSE and International A Level can be sat online.

**If the learner can get to a centre, the centre exam is cheaper: always offer it** ([Pass GCSE](#/kb/p?slug=pmg-at-a-glance) or [Pass A Level](#/kb/p?slug=alevel-at-a-glance)).

## Say this

- "Pearson Edexcel International GCSE" or "International A Level". If they say IGCSE, check it's Pearson Edexcel, not Cambridge.
- "Internationally recognised qualification." International GCSEs are graded 9 to 1 like GCSE and accepted by colleges, universities and most employers: "check your course".
- "UK universities accept International A Levels grade for grade."
- "We enter you with Pearson and send you everything you need. You do Pearson's familiarisation at home, in your own time, within Pearson's window."
- "Online exams run in the Summer and November series (International GCSE), and January, Summer and October (International A Level)."

## Never say

- "We're the only (or first) provider."
- "Ofqual regulated." It isn't: say internationally recognised.
- "You can sit it any time of year."
- "All International GCSEs can be sat online." Cambridge can't.
- "You can sit your AQA or OCR GCSE online." UK GCSEs and A Levels need a centre.
- "Home invigilation." That's a different service (an invigilator in the home) that isn't on sale. This is remote invigilation.
- "We'll do a tech call with you." There's no call: the learner does Pearson's familiarisation and system check themselves.
- "You can sit mocks remotely." Mocks are sat at a centre.
- Tutors & Exams' own prices or deadlines.
