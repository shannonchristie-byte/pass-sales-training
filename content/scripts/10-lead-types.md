## On every call

- If they don't answer, leave a voicemail. PFS template: [Voicemails, providers and Get Into Uni](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-calls-referrals).
- If you speak but don't sell, set the callback so it moves to your own list.
- If you sell, fill in the sale form on the CRM.
- Before taking payment, ask permission to tick the terms and conditions box for them. They get a copy with their order confirmation.
- After payment, tell them the order confirmation and terms and conditions will arrive within 15 minutes. If they don't, they should call back.

## Talking about price

Always back up why it costs what it does. The course gives them everything they need to pass, with no other revision materials needed. The exam price covers the exam, the invigilator, results, and certificates (digital and physical). We're the UK's leading Functional Skills provider, at the most competitive prices.
