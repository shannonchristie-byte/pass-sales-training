Policy behind this call: [Funded courses](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-funded). Prices: [PFS prices](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-prices).

## 1. Introduce yourself and build rapport

> "I'm calling from Pass Functional Skills about the funded Functional Skills course you enquired about. Are the qualifications something you still need? What do you need them for: university, work? When do you need them by?"

Reassure them it's definitely something we can help with.

## 2. Explain Functional Skills Level 2

> "It's equivalent to a GCSE grade C, or grade 4 in the new GCSEs, and it's Ofqual regulated, so it's accredited and recognised. For (nursing, university and so on), it's perfect for you."

## 3. Explain the course

> "The course is normally £355 without our current 20% off, and that's what we look to cover if you qualify."

> **To check:** £355 here, but the PFS doc says the course is about £284. Which should the script use?

It includes:

- **Diagnostics:** the Initial Assessment and Subject Knowledge Assessment
- **Video demonstrations**
- **Practice questions**
- **Mock exam questions**
- **Progress tracking:** "It shows how far through the course you are, which keeps you motivated, and your average scores on practice questions and mocks. The real exam's pass mark is around 55%, so if you're getting 60% or more, that's a really good sign you're ready. It stops you going in too early and gives you confidence."
- A **93% success rate** when the course is completed
- **24/7 access**, as quickly or slowly as they like. We never put pressure on anyone to finish by a certain time.

## 4. Explain the exam

> "It's done at home, online, just like the course. Maths is one 2-hour exam. English has three parts: reading and writing in one 2-hour sitting (an hour each), then speaking, usually on a different day, for about an hour."

> "The exam is a cost everyone pays themselves, because each person is entered with the exam board as an individual candidate. That's how you get the full Ofqual-regulated qualification. It's £269, or £215.20 with our current offer, per subject, and that includes the exam, results and certificate, so there's nothing more to pay."

## 5. See if they qualify

> "This is just so we can understand your situation and see what support we can give."

- Do they work? Part-time or full-time?
- Do they get any benefits, Universal Credit or other support?
- Do they have children, or anyone they look after?
- Confirm what the qualification is for, and the deadline.

**If they qualify:**

> "Great, I'm more than happy to put you through for the funding. That means we pay the whole £284 the course currently costs, so you get full access to the premium revision course, and you just cover the exam cost."

**If they don't:** explain why (funding goes to people not working, with children, on benefits and so on), reassure them we'll still help them get their qualification, and offer another route: a bundle or a payment plan.

Ask how that sounds and whether they have any questions.

## 6. Close

> "Moving forward, it's just a case of getting you registered. The day you register, you get the course, so you can start revising today. The exam is on what's called book now, choose the date later, so it's ready whenever you are, but you don't pick the date and time until you've revised and feel ready. Shall we get you started today?"

> "Would you like to get the exam paid off in full today, to get it out of the way?"

If not, go down the payment plan route. If they ask to pay for the exam nearer the time: the funded course only comes as a bundle with the exam, so we can only set up the funding with an exam booking.

## 7. Confirm and take payment

> "Just to confirm, this is for your Ofqual-regulated Functional Skills (maths/English) Level 2. We're fully funding the premium course, and you're covering the exam. The course is available to start today, and the exam is on book now, choose the date later, so you only pick the date when you're ready."

Take card details. Tell them the order confirmation and terms and conditions will arrive within 15 minutes, and to call back if they don't.
