Check WooCommerce first: it may be a resit, or they may have bought the course separately. See [Lead types](#/kb/p?slug=scripts-lead-types).

## 1. Introduce yourself

> "Hi there, it's (name) calling from A Star Equivalency, about the GCSE equivalency exam in (subject) you've booked with us. What do you need it for: university, PGCE, nursing? Do you have a deadline?"

## 2. Reassure them

> "We help thousands of learners in the same situation every year. We'll support you as much as you need to get the right GCSE equivalencies for your course by your deadline."

> "Do you have any questions about the exams?"

## 3. Why book the course now

> "Providers and universities much prefer people to have their GCSE equivalencies when they apply, so it gives you a stronger chance of an offer. And the longer you give yourself to revise, the better: it takes the pressure off rushing."

Explain the course: [The courses](#/kb/p?slug=astar-courses).

## 4. Close

> "Moving forward, it's just a case of getting you registered, and you'll have the course ready to start revising. Your exam is already booked. Shall we get that registered for you today, so you can make a start as soon as possible?"

> **To check:** the doc says the course is "sent out" and takes about 3 working days, but the A Star course is online, with access straight away. Which is right?

> "Just to confirm, this is for your (subject) GCSE equivalency course. Perfect. Could I start by taking the card number you'd like to pay with?"

**If they hesitate:** offer 10% off the course, or a demo to show them their account and talk it through again. If they're happy after that, register them today.
