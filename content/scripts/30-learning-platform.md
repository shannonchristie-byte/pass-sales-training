For people who signed up to the free version of the Functional Skills course. Check WooCommerce first: see [Lead types](#/kb/p?slug=scripts-lead-types).

## 1. Introduce yourself and build rapport

> "Hi, is that (name)? Hello, I'm calling from Pass Functional Skills about Functional Skills (maths/English) qualifications. I can see you've registered for the free version of our course on the website, so I was calling to see if you need to get these qualifications. What do you need them for: university, work? When do you need them by?"

## 2. Reassure them

> "That sounds great. It's definitely something we can help with, and get your qualifications by your deadline."

> "Functional Skills Level 2 is equivalent to a GCSE grade C, or grade 4 in the new GCSEs, and it's Ofqual regulated, so it's accredited and recognised. For (nursing, university and so on), it's perfect for you."

## 3. Ask about the free version, then explain the premium course

> "Have you had much of a look at the free version yet? How are you finding it?"

> "The upgraded version gives you the full premium course, with everything you need to revise, prepare and pass. When our course is completed, we have a 93% success rate."

- **Revise:** videos of an expert tutor explaining the key terms, the knowledge and how to apply it to exam questions, which can be watched as often as needed and get more in depth as they go.
- **Practice questions:** easy, medium and hard multiple choice, each marked with the correct answer and a written and video solution, as often as they like.
- **Topic tests:** typed answers, closer to the real exam, marked the same way.
- **Practice papers:** maths has plenty of full papers. See [The courses](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-courses).
- **Progress tracking:** how far through they are, and their average scores. "The pass mark is around 55%, so 60% or more is a really good sign you're ready."

## 4. Explain the exam

> "Would you be booking the exam with us too, or do you already have it booked elsewhere?"

> "The exams are also done at home, online. Maths is one 2-hour exam, with non-calculator and calculator sections. English has three parts: reading and writing in one 2-hour sitting (an hour each), then speaking on a different day, for about an hour."

## 5. Questions

> "Do you have any questions about anything we've gone through?"

Answer them, and reassure them this is the best option and we'll support them as much as they need. We help hundreds of people in the same situation.

## 6. Close

> "Moving forward, it's just a case of getting you registered. The day you register, you get the full premium course, so you can start revising today. The exam is on book now, choose the date later, so it's ready whenever you are. Shall we get you started today?"

If they push back on price, back up why it costs what it does (see [Lead types](#/kb/p?slug=scripts-lead-types)).

## 7. Confirm and take payment

> "Just to confirm, this is for your Ofqual-regulated Functional Skills (maths/English) (course/exam)."

Don't mention payment plans unless they ask, or seem worried or hesitant about paying. If they do, go down the payment plan route, or offer a discount if needed.
