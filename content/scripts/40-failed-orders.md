Check WooCommerce first, and check nobody in the room is already dealing with them: see [Lead types](#/kb/p?slug=scripts-lead-types).

> "Hi, is that (name)? I'm calling from (Pass Functional Skills / A Star Equivalency). I can see you were trying to put a booking through for the (product), but it hasn't gone through. Did you have any problems, or is there anything about the (course/exam) you're wondering about before registering?"

- **Answer their questions.** Most will say they were looking at prices. Use that to reassure them how good the course or exam is, and explain everything they get for the price.
- **If the price was too high:** is there a discount you can offer if they book with you now? Did they know about payment plans?
- **If the course was in their basket:** explain what the course includes and how it works.
- **If the exam was in their basket:** explain how exam day works. The price includes the full exam, the invigilator, results and certificates, and for Functional Skills it's all Ofqual regulated.

Then close as normal: confirm what they're buying, ask permission to tick the terms and conditions, and take payment.
