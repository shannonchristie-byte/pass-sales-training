Check A Star WooCommerce first: see [Lead types](#/kb/p?slug=scripts-lead-types). These are support calls as much as sales calls. See also the resit list on [A Star discounts](#/kb/p?slug=astar-discounts).

## 1. Introduce yourself

> "Hi there, it's (name) calling from A Star Equivalency, about the GCSE equivalency exam in (subject) you sat with us a while ago. I can see that unfortunately you didn't pass that attempt, so we're calling to see how we can support you and make sure you still get your qualification."

- "Is this qualification still something you need?"
- "What do you need it for: university, PGCE, nursing?"
- "Do you have a deadline?"

## 2. Reassure them

> "We help thousands of learners in the same situation every year. We'll support you as much as you need to get the right GCSE equivalencies for your course by your deadline. We can put together a revision plan and answer any questions."

## 3. The course

Check WooCommerce for whether they had the course before. If not:

> "We'd always strongly advise our full course to make sure you pass. When it's completed we have a 93% success rate, so it's the best revision you can use. It's an online learning portal covering everything you need to revise, prepare and pass."

## 4. The exam

> "You'll know the exams from last time, but just to recap: they're online over Google Meet, with a human invigilator who helps you get set up and is there throughout if you have any problems."

Open the subject page and talk through the exam's length and structure. Results take 10 working days, with a digital certificate. If they're in a rush, suggest **fast track**: results in 2 working days.

> "Do you have any questions about the exams?"

## 5. What we can do to help with their resit

- A discount on the course if they book their resit and don't have the course yet
- Half-price, or even free, fast track if they're in a rush
- A provider letter if their provider or university is pressuring them. It often takes the pressure off and gives them time to resit

## 6. Why book now

> "Providers and universities much prefer people to have their GCSE equivalencies when they apply, so it gives you a stronger chance of an offer. And the longer you give yourself to revise, the better: it takes the pressure off rushing."

## 7. Close

> "Shall we get that registered for you today, so you can make a start as soon as possible?"

> "Just to confirm, this is for your (subject) GCSE equivalency, for the full (course/exam). Perfect. Could I start by taking the card number you'd like to pay with?"

**If they hesitate:** offer the early bird offer (10% off the course if they book the course and exam together), or a demo to show them their account and talk it through again. If they're happy after that, register them today.
