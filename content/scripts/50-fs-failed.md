Check WooCommerce first, and book through their existing account: see [Lead types](#/kb/p?slug=scripts-lead-types).

## 1. Introduce yourself

> "Hi, is that (name)? I'm calling from the Pass Functional Skills support team about the Functional Skills (maths/English) exam you sat with us."

> "Have you had your results back yet?"

**If they haven't had their results: suggest they check their emails, and end the call there. Never tell them they've failed.**

## 2. Empathise and build rapport

> "I'm really sorry you didn't get the grade you needed this time. Exams can be overwhelming. Please be reassured we can still support you and make sure you get your qualification."

- "Is this qualification still something you need?"
- "What do you need it for: university, nursing?"
- "Do you have a deadline?"

> "We help hundreds of learners in the same situation every year. We'll support you as much as you need to make sure you're on track for the qualification you need."

## 3. Find out what happened

> "How did you find the exam day? Were there any problems, or did it just not go to plan? What were you using to revise?"

> "I know it's disheartening not to get the grade first time, but we'll help you pass next time, so you can go on to (university, nursing, a new job)."

## 4. If they didn't have our course, recommend it

> "We always strongly advise our premium course for Functional Skills exams. It covers everything you need to revise and pass, and when it's completed we have a 93% success rate, so you can be confident you're using the best materials."

Explain revise, practice questions, topic tests and progress tracking. See [The courses](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-courses).

## 5. The exam

> "You'll know the exam process from last time. Are you happy with it, or would you like me to go over it again?"

Resit rules: [Access arrangements, resits and support](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-access-resits).

## 6. Questions, then close

> "Do you have any questions about anything we've gone through?"

> "Moving forward, it's just a case of getting you registered. The day you register, you get the full premium course, so you can start revising today. The exam is on book now, choose the date later, so it's ready whenever you are (or we can pick an exact date if you need one). Shall we get you started today?"

Back up the price if needed (see [Lead types](#/kb/p?slug=scripts-lead-types)).

## 7. Confirm and take payment

> "Just to confirm, this is for your Ofqual-regulated Functional Skills (maths/English) (course/exam)."

Don't mention payment plans unless they ask or seem hesitant. Then take their details and get them registered.
