Check WooCommerce first: it may be a resit, or they may have bought the course separately. See [Lead types](#/kb/p?slug=scripts-lead-types).

## 1. Introduce yourself

> "Hi there, it's (name) calling from Pass Functional Skills, about the Functional Skills (subject) exam you've booked with us. What do you need it for: university, nursing? Do you have a deadline?"

## 2. Reassure them

> "We help thousands of learners in the same situation every year. We'll support you as much as you need to get the Functional Skills qualification you need for your course, by your deadline."

## 3. Ask about revision and recommend the course

> "I can see you booked the exam only, so I was wondering what you're using to revise at the moment?"

> "We always strongly advise our full premium course for these exams. When it's completed we have a 93% success rate, so it's the best revision you can use to be fully prepared and pass."

Explain the course in detail: the Initial Assessment, videos, practice questions, mock tests and progress tracking. See [The courses](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-courses).

> "Do you have any questions about the course or the revision side?"

## 4. Reassure them about their exam

Go over the type of invigilation they've booked, how it works and the results time. See [Exams and exam boards](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-exams).

> "Do you have any questions about the exams?"

## 5. Why start now

> "Providers and universities much prefer people to have their Functional Skills qualifications when they apply, so it gives you a stronger chance of an offer. And the longer you give yourself to revise, the better: it takes the pressure off rushing."

## 6. Close

> "Moving forward, it's just a case of getting you registered. The day you register, you get the course, so you can start revising today. Shall we get you started?"

> "Just to confirm, this is for your Ofqual-regulated Functional Skills (maths/English) Level 2 course. It'll be available to start today."

Try to take payment in full without mentioning payment plans, unless they ask or seem worried or hesitant. If not, go down the payment plan route. Take card details. Finish by asking if they have any questions, and wish them luck with their revision and exam.
