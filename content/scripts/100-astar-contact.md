The standard A Star sales call. Before you start, check the teaching route: [A Star at a glance](#/kb/p?slug=astar-at-a-glance).

## 1. Introduce yourself and build rapport

> "Hi there, it's (name) calling from A Star Equivalency, about the GCSE-equivalent qualifications you enquired about. Are they still something you need?"

- "What do you need them for: university, PGCE, nursing?"
- "Which GCSE equivalencies are you looking for: maths, English, Biology, Combined Science?"
- "Do you have a deadline?"

## 2. Reassure them

> "We help thousands of learners in the same situation every year. We'll support you as much as you need to get the right GCSE equivalencies for your course by your deadline. We can put together a revision plan and answer any questions."

## 3. The course

> "You get the full premium revision course: an online course covering every topic that could come up in the exam, with video demonstrations, practice questions, mock exams and progress tracking. When it's completed we have a 93% success rate, so it's the best revision you can use."

> "Do you have any questions about the course or the revision side?"

## 4. The exams

> "The exams are online over Google Meet, with a human invigilator who helps you get set up and is there throughout if you have any problems."

Open the subject page and talk through the exam's length and structure: [Exams](#/kb/p?slug=astar-exams). Results take 10 working days, with a digital certificate. If they're in a rush, suggest **fast track**: results in 2 working days.

> "Do you have any questions about the exams?"

## 5. Why book now

> "Providers and universities much prefer people to have their GCSE equivalencies when they apply, so it gives you a stronger chance of an offer. And the longer you give yourself to revise, the better: it takes the pressure off rushing."

## 6. Close

> "Moving forward, it's just a case of getting you registered. The day you register, you get instant access to the course, so you can start revising whenever you're ready. The exam is on book now, choose the date later, so it's booked for whenever you need it, but you don't pick the date and time until you've revised and feel ready. Shall we get that registered for you today?"

> "Just to confirm, this is for your (subjects) GCSE equivalency, for the full (course/exam). Perfect. Could I start by taking the card number you'd like to pay with?"

**If they hesitate:** offer the early bird offer (10% off the course if they book the course and exam together), or a demo to show them their account and talk it through again: [Running a demo](#/kb/p?slug=astar-demos). If they're happy after that, register them today.
