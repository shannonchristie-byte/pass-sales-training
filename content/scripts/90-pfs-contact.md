For the PFS main contact form, callback requests, free test requests and "find a centre" forms. It's the standard PFS sales call.

## 1. Introduce yourself and build rapport

> "Hi, is that (name)? I'm calling from Pass Functional Skills about the Functional Skills (course/exam) you enquired about. Are the qualifications something you still need? What do you need them for: university, work? When do you need them by?"

## 2. Reassure them and relate to their situation

> "That sounds great. It's definitely something we can help with, and make sure you have your qualifications by your deadline."

> "We help hundreds of people in the same situation every year. Nursing and university students are the biggest groups we support, so this will be great for you."

> "You may already know, but Functional Skills Level 2 is equivalent to a GCSE grade C, or grade 4 in the new GCSEs, and it's Ofqual regulated, so it's accredited and recognised. For (nursing, university and so on), it's perfect for you."

## 3. Recommend the bundle and explain the course

> "From everything we've discussed, the best thing for you is our course and exam bundle, which gives you everything you need."

> "The full premium course is online, so you can use it anywhere, 24/7, as quickly or slowly as you like, around your other commitments."

- It starts with a **Subject Knowledge Assessment**, a mini test showing which topics they're stronger and weaker on, to plan their revision.
- **Revise:** videos of an expert tutor explaining the key terms, the knowledge and how to apply it to exam questions.
- **Practice questions:** easy, medium and hard multiple choice, each marked with written and video solutions.
- **Topic tests:** typed answers, closer to the real exam, marked the same way.
- **Mock exams** in practice mode (untimed, marked as they go) or exam mode (timed, marked at the end). Maths has plenty of papers, and they don't have to do them all.
- **Progress tracking:** how far through they are, and their average scores. "The pass mark is around 55%, so 60% or more is a really good sign you're ready."
- A **93% success rate** when the course is completed.

## 4. Explain the exam

> "It's done at home, online, like the course. Maths is one 2-hour exam with non-calculator and calculator sections. English has three parts: reading and writing in one 2-hour sitting, then speaking on a different day, for about an hour."

Results depend on the invigilation:

- **Automated** (VQA (TQUK)): a secure online portal, recorded, with results in 2 to 6 working days
- **Human** (Open Awards): over Zoom, with results in 6 to 16 working days

> "Do you have any questions about the course or the exam?"

Answer anything they're unsure about, and stress how much support they'll get from us throughout.

## 5. The value of the bundle

> "Like I mentioned, the bundle is the best option. Bought separately, the course and exam would come to more. With the bundle you get the full premium course and the exam for less, and the premium bundle includes a free resit, so if anything goes wrong on the day you have a safety net and don't pay again."

Use today's prices from [PFS prices](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-prices).

> **To check:** the doc says "the course on its own is £355 and the exams are £269, so around £600 or more separately". Confirm which figures to quote.

## 6. Close

> "Moving forward, it's just a case of getting you registered. The day you register, you get the course, so you can start revising today. The exam is on book now, choose the date later, so it's ready whenever you are. Shall we get you started today?"

If they object, use your options: the basic bundle, 10% off the course, payment plans. See [Objection handling](#/kb/s?key=objections).

## 7. Confirm and take payment

> "Just to confirm, this is for your Ofqual-regulated Functional Skills (maths/English) Level 2. The course is available to start today, and the exam is on book now, choose the date later, so you only pick the date when you're ready."

- Try to take payment in full without mentioning payment plans, unless they ask or seem hesitant. If not, go down the payment plan route.
- **Before taking payment, ask permission to tick the terms and conditions box for them.** They get a copy with their order confirmation.
- Take card details. Tell them the order is placed, and the confirmation and terms will arrive within 15 minutes, or they should call back.
- Finish by asking if they have any questions, and wish them luck.
