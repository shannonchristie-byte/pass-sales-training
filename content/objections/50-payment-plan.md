## Lines to use

> "No problem, it's simple. Klarna is 3 equal payments a month apart, starting today. Clearpay is 4 payments, every 2 weeks. PayPal is 3 monthly payments. All three are interest free."

> "You don't have to finish paying before you sit your exam or get your results."

## What to do

- Paying in full is always preferred. Bring up splitting the cost only once they've said they can't pay upfront.
- **Payl8r is finance with interest.** Only say "We're partnered with Payl8r, a finance provider" and point them to the calculator on our website.
- **Never:** recommend Payl8r, say what it would cost a month, help them apply, or suggest they'll be approved.
- A Level orders of £999 or more can also use Partial.ly. See [A Level prices](#/kb/p?slug=alevel-prices).
- Full details: [PFS payment options](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-payment).
