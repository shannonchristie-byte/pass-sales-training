## Lines to use

> "Does that price include VAT? Many providers add 20% at checkout."

> "Is it Ofqual regulated? Ours always is."

> "Most providers won't let you sit the exam until you've paid in full. With us you can."

## What to do

- Ask for the link and check it's like for like before going any further.
- Use the comparison tables: [PFS against other providers](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-at-a-glance) and [A Star against Equivalency Testing](#/kb/p?slug=astar-at-a-glance).
