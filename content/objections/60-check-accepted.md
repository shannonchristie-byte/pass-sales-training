## Lines to use

> "That's the right thing to do. I'd rather you check than buy something that doesn't fit."

> "Ask them which level and subjects they need, and whether they accept Functional Skills."

For Access to HE:

> "Check the course page or ask admissions whether they accept Access to HE for that course, and whether they need specific grades, units, GCSEs, Functional Skills or work experience."

## What to do

- Book a callback for after they've checked.
- **School teacher training:** Functional Skills Level 2 isn't automatically accepted, so point them to GCSE or A Star equivalency instead.
- For A Star, check the "does your university accept" list.
- A free provider letter can help keep an offer open while they're booked in.
