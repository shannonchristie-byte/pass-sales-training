## Lines to use

> "When do you need the qualification by?"

Then work back from that date, allowing for results times (TQUK about 6 working days).

> "If you book now you can start the course straight away and choose your exam date later, so you're not locked in."

> "If you've got a deadline, we can send a free letter confirming your exam is booked, which helps keep a university or job offer open."

For "I don't get paid until…": offer the deposit route or interest-free instalments, possibly with a discount, to get them over the line there and then.

## What to do

- Find their reason to start now: a deadline, an application or a start date. If there isn't one, see [Urgency and discounts](#/kb/p?slug=objection-urgency-discounts).
- Offer the deposit route only once they've said they can't pay in full today.
- **A Levels:** the late fees from the end of January are a real deadline. See the [A Level call guide](#/kb/p?slug=alevel-call-guide).
- If they still won't go ahead, move to support. Offer a free demo of the course and exam, and book a personal callback with a date.
