This is as written in the General sales policy and the PFS policy.

- **Find an urgency signal:** a deadline for a university course or job application, or simply wanting to get it done.
- **If there's no urgency, create it.** If a sale is on, imply it expires shortly: "sign up now to get the discount". If no sale is on, create a specific discount with a logical reason. For example, for someone applying to the police: "we work with police constabularies around the country, so there's a special discount because of the numbers we support."
- **"I don't get paid until…":** offer the deposit route or interest-free instalments, possibly with a discount, to get them over the line there and then.
- **Discounts:** offer one only if they ask or you'd lose the sale. Make them feel special, with a reason it applies to them (such as blue light), and say it's for today only. If they want a callback, say you can put the request in but can't promise it will be granted.
- **Exam bookings:** a date further ahead can bring the total down.
- **Where discounts apply:** only to course-only orders or the course part of a bundle.
- **If they won't sign up there and then:** ease off, move to support, and offer a free demo.
