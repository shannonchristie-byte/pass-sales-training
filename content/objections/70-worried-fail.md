## Lines to use

> "Our success rate is 93%, and the course tells you when you're exam ready."

> "There are plenty of full mock papers. Maths Level 2 has 60. The premium bundle also includes a free resit."

For Access to HE:

> "That's understandable. It's a challenging course, and we don't want to hide that: it's Level 3, equivalent to 3 A Levels. But it's designed for adult learners, with learning materials, assignment guidance, feedback and tutor support. The main thing is whether you can commit the time consistently and stay motivated."

## What to do

- **Access to HE:** be honest that it's Level 3 and around 600 hours, and check they can commit the time.
- **A Star and GCSE maths:** advise foundation unless they need grade 6 or above. It has much higher pass rates.
- Someone who has been out of education a long time: see the Access [call guide](#/kb/p?slug=access-call-guide) for how to reassure them.
