Mostly PFS. For A Star and GCSE maths, the same question is foundation or higher: see [A Star exams](#/kb/p?slug=astar-exams).

## Lines to use

> "What do you need it for? University, nursing and most jobs ask for Level 2, which is equivalent to a GCSE grade 4, the old grade C."

> "The course starts with a 30-minute Initial Assessment that shows where you are now and which level we'd recommend. It's a guide, not a pass or fail."

> "The Subject Knowledge Assessment then builds a personal learning plan, so you spend your time on the topics you need."

## What to do

- Ask them to confirm the exact level and subjects their course or employer needs.
- We cover maths and English at Entry Level 3, Level 1 and Level 2. Advise Level 2 when that's what they need.
- **Teaching:** check the route first. Most teacher training (PGCE) needs GCSE equivalency, not Functional Skills. See [A Star at a glance](#/kb/p?slug=astar-at-a-glance).
