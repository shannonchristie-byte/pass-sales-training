## Lines to use

> "I understand. Our prices include VAT, where a lot of providers add 20% at checkout, and every exam we offer is Ofqual regulated."

> "Exam prices work a bit like flights: the further ahead you book, the cheaper it can be. If you don't need it straight away, a later date brings the total down."

> "If you don't need 12 months' access or the free resit, the basic bundle is a cheaper option."

For Access to HE:

> "I completely understand. It's a big decision. Bear in mind this is a full Level 3 qualification, equivalent to 3 A Levels, designed to support progression into university and long-term career goals."

## What to do

- **Only talk about price once you've built rapport.**
- Offer a discount only if you'd lose the sale otherwise, and only on the course part. Give a reason that applies to them, such as blue light, and make it for today only (see [Urgency and discounts](#/kb/p?slug=objection-urgency-discounts)).
- A discount costs stars as well as revenue: a 10% code takes a PFS course from 4 stars to 3.5.
- Each service's prices and limits: [PFS](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-prices), [A Star](#/kb/p?slug=astar-prices), [Pass GCSE](#/kb/p?slug=pmg-prices), [A Level](#/kb/p?slug=alevel-prices), [Access to HE](#/kb/p?slug=access-prices).
