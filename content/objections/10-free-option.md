Mostly PFS. For Access to HE and A Levels, see their call guides.

## Lines to use

> "The exam always has a fee, because each person is entered individually with the exam board. That's what makes it an Ofqual-regulated qualification."

> "A completely free route is through a local college, but that usually means waiting for their start dates and going in person. With us you can start today and sit the exam online from home."

If they asked about funding:

> "The course part can sometimes be covered. Can I ask a couple of quick questions to see if you qualify?"

## What to do

- **Funded course criteria:** not working or part-time, on Universal Credit, or caring for dependants. Offer a discount first. Funding for someone who doesn't meet the criteria is a last resort to save the sale. Full rules: [Funded courses](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-funded).
- With a funded course, the exam is still paid and can't be discounted. It can go on a payment plan or the deposit route, at least the deposit is paid before the course starts, and there's no free resit.
- Someone on the **free version of the course**: talk about what the full course adds.
- Comparing us with college: use the table on [Funded courses](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-funded).
