## Access arrangements

We apply to the exam board directly when a learner needs extra time, a reader or a scribe, for example. What's available depends on the board. Once booked, the learner sends evidence (a diagnostic letter, a doctor's letter or an EHCP) to the exams team at [help@passfunctionalskills.co.uk](mailto:help@passfunctionalskills.co.uk).

The boards need time to approve and apply arrangements:

| Exam board | What's available | Time to approve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VQA (TQUK) | 25% extra time only | 6 working days |
| Open Awards | All access arrangements | 10 working days |
| City and Guilds | 25% extra time only | 20 working days |

### Exemption from the SLC

In extreme circumstances, beyond the usual reasons for access arrangements (for example, a learner who is mute), a learner can be made exempt from the English SLC.

- The exam board needs **full medical evidence**. A doctor's note isn't enough, but an EHCP is a good option.
- If it's approved, the certificate states they didn't complete the speaking, listening and communication element.
- We can't guarantee every university or course will accept it. We haven't heard of one that hasn't, but it's case by case.

## Resits

- A learner can retake an exam any time after getting the results of the last one.
- **VQA (TQUK):** 3 resits until the next cycle. **Open Awards and City and Guilds:** up to 5. After that they'd need to change exam board.
- **Maths:** the whole exam is resat.
- **English:** only the failed components are resat, not all three.
- If they need results by a certain date, ask what the date is and work back from it to pick an exam that allows for the results time.

## Merging components (credit transfer)

- For English, learners can do different components with different exam boards, and we organise a credit transfer.
- Merging component passes into the full qualification costs a fee (see [PFS prices](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-prices)) and usually takes 5 working days.
- **VQA (TQUK)** doesn't issue component certificates. It only completes a credit transfer if the learner holds a pass for one of the components.
- **Open Awards and City and Guilds** give a component certificate or evidence of passing, which the learner can take elsewhere.

## Provider letters

A headed, signed letter from us confirming the exam is booked and how long results should take. It also says that passing gives them their Ofqual-regulated Functional Skills Level 2.

Letters often take the pressure off. They're proof the learner is on track, which helps stop university or job offers being withdrawn. **They're free for anyone under time pressure who books with us.**
