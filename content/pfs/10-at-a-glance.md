Pass Functional Skills (PFS) is the UK's leading provider of Functional Skills qualifications. Nursing is the biggest sector we support, and Functional Skills will probably make up about 80% of your calls.

## What Functional Skills is

- **Level 2** is equivalent to a GCSE grade C, or grade 4 on the new GCSE scale.
- With PFS it is **Ofqual regulated**, so it is accredited and recognised.
- Functional Skills only exists in **maths, English and digital skills**.
- We offer courses and exams for maths and English at **Entry Level 3, Level 1 and Level 2**. We do Level 1, but we advise Level 2.
- Digital Skills Level 1 is a **course only**, with no exam.

> **To check:** the doc says Level 2 is "accepted by 100% of universities, apprenticeships and employers, guaranteed". Pass's own teaching guide says Functional Skills Level 2 is not automatically accepted for school teacher training. Agree the wording before this page is marked as checked.

## Key selling points

1. **Speed from booking to exam.** Exams run 365 days a year, 24 hours a day.
2. **Speed of results.** We're registered with 3 major awarding bodies (exam boards) and can pick the fastest.
3. **Progress tracking** with course access.
4. **Ofqual regulated.**
5. **Flexible, self-paced course.**
6. **Price:** lowest cost guaranteed for a like-for-like product.
7. **Free resit** with the premium bundle.
8. **Free demo** of the course and exam for those who need it.
9. **We work directly with universities and training providers** across many sectors.
10. **93% pass rate** for those who complete the course. As long as the learner puts the effort in, they should pass.

## Functional Skills or GCSE?

Many learners think they want, or have to take, the full GCSE. We do offer GCSEs through Pass GCSE. But for maths or English Language, strongly advise Functional Skills instead.

| Functional Skills | GCSE |
| --- | --- |
| Online exams at home | In person at an exam centre |
| Exams 365 days a year, 24/7 | Set dates only: May/June, with resits in November |
| Easier content | Harder content |
| Much cheaper (see [PFS prices](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-prices)) | More expensive |

The advantages of Functional Skills Level 2 over GCSE: it's cheaper, quicker, done at home and easier to pass.

## Good to know

- **Provider letter:** once someone has booked, we can write a letter saying they're booked in for an Ofqual-regulated exam and waiting for results. It can help them get a university or job deadline extended. See [Access arrangements, resits and support](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-access-resits).
- **Exam only still gets materials:** with an exam-only booking we still post out mock papers and revision materials. Mention this if the learner has ruled out the course.
- **They can take the exam whenever they're ready.**
- **Home-schooled students:** parents sometimes ask whether our qualifications count as full-time education. For that, they apply to their local authority and get it signed off. We can provide any evidence that they're on the course to support the application.
