Four ways to buy, in the order to offer them. Prices for each are on [PFS prices](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-prices).

## 1. Premium bundle: always offer this first

- The premium course for **12 months**
- The full exam
- **One free resit**: the whole exam for maths, and one component for English
- It's cheaper than buying the course and exam separately

To bring the price down, pick an exam date at the time of booking. The cheapest dates are 4 months ahead.

## 2. Basic bundle: the fallback

Offer it once the premium bundle has been explored. It suits someone who wants a cheaper option, or doesn't need 12 months' access or the free resit, for example someone in a rush or very confident.

- **3 months** of course access, instead of 12
- The exam
- **No free resit**

As with the premium bundle, picking a date 4 months ahead brings the price down.

## 3. Course only

Only the full premium course, with 12 months' access. It can be reduced in certain situations, for example:

- They've already booked the exam elsewhere or are taking it at college
- They booked the exam with us, and we're calling nearer the exam to help with revision
- Their course access has run out and they want to keep revising

## 4. Exam only

The exam with no course material. We don't usually advise it, but it suits someone confident of passing, someone who needs the exam as soon as possible and has no time to revise, or someone who has already revised with our course.

They can pick a date when booking or use book now, choose the date later. The price depends on the date and the exam board.

**Even with exam only, we post out mock papers and revision materials.** Mention this if they've ruled out the course.

## Discounts

The most you can take off is **10% of the course part**, on top of the website sale price. Discounts never apply to the exam. See [Discounts and offers](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-discounts).
