## Voicemail

> "Hi (name), it's (your name) calling from Pass Functional Skills about the enquiry you left for Functional Skills qualifications. I understand this is for (their reason: university, a job and so on), which is definitely something we can help with! I'll drop you an email. If you reply and let me know a better day and time for a callback, we can go through everything and answer any questions. Thank you, goodbye."

After 3 unanswered calls there's an email template in the CRM: **PFS 3rd call no answer (funded)**.

## Provider leads

The business development team works with providers (workplaces, apprenticeship providers, alternative provision, charities and others) on contracts for batches of course and exam licences. These leads often reach sales first. Take these details and pass them to the provider team:

- Name
- Email
- Phone number
- Number of learners
- A good time to contact them

## Get Into Uni referrals

**Only bring this up after the sale is complete, payment has gone through and the normal post-sale confirmation is done.** It isn't part of the sale and must never be used to pressure anyone into buying.

Get Into Uni is a partner company that helps adult learners into higher education, free to learners booked with us. They can help with:

- Understanding university options and the right route
- Finding a university and course
- The UCAS application
- Student finance guidance
- Interview preparation
- Support from first enquiry to enrolment

It gives learners a clear next step, makes Pass feel more helpful, and gives them somewhere to take questions about courses, funding, UCAS and interviews.

### Who can be referred

Only qualified leads. All of these must be true:

- They've completed their booking and payment with us
- They've said the qualification is for university, or a university-related route
- They're looking at university in **London, Manchester or Birmingham**
- They can travel to campus **at least 1 day a week**
- They're interested in speaking to Get Into Uni
- They give permission for their details to be passed on
- The referral form is completed in full and accurately

### The script

1. **Finish the post-sale confirmation:** "You'll receive your order confirmation, a copy of the Terms and Conditions and everything you need by email."
2. **Confirm university intent:** "Just remind me, you mentioned you wanted to use this to go on to university, is that right?"
3. **Introduce Get Into Uni:** "We have a partner company called Get Into Uni. They specialise in supporting learners who want to go on to university. They offer completely free advice and guidance through the whole process: finding a university and course, the UCAS application, student finance and interview preparation if needed. It's a free bolt-on for learners booked in with us. Would you like me to pass your details over so they can arrange a call with you?"
4. **Get consent and complete the referral:** "Great, I'll just take a few extra details so I can pass this across properly. They'll then contact you to talk through your university options and next steps."

### Submitting it

1. Complete every field on the [referral form](https://getintouni.aidaform.com/pass-referral-form-questionnaire) accurately. It asks for the subject they want to study, whether they're 21 or over, the city or university, whether they can travel to campus weekly, their highest qualification (UK or overseas), residency and whether they've lived in the UK for the last 3 years, any previous student finance and for how many years, whether they've worked at least 2 of the last 5 years, and when they'd like to start.
2. Submit it, and it goes to Get Into Uni for follow-up.
3. Log the half star in the CRM: product **Other**, with the note **Get Into Uni referral**.

**Quality over volume.** A qualified referral earns 0.5 stars, but Get Into Uni scores lead quality. Never submit weak or unqualified leads for the half star.

### What learners say

| Learner says | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "I'm not sure what course or university yet" | That's exactly what Get Into Uni can help with. Explain what they offer and ask if they'd like a free call. If yes, complete the form |
| "Yes, I'm looking for a university place" | Explain Get Into Uni, ask for consent and complete the form in full |
| "I'm worried about funding" | Get Into Uni can give student finance guidance, but don't promise they'll be eligible. Ask if they'd like a call |
| "I don't want anyone to contact me" | No problem. Don't submit a referral |
