All our Functional Skills exams are online. There's no paper option. Exams run 365 days a year, 24 hours a day, at the most competitive price for a like-for-like service and with the quickest results.

**Strongly advise VQA (TQUK)**, our main exam board. We also offer Open Awards, and City and Guilds if the learner needs it.

## Two ways to book an exam

- **Book now, choose the date later.** The exam is secured, and they pick the date when they're ready. They have 12 months to choose a date, but the exam itself doesn't have to be sat within the 12 months.
- **Pick a date now.** The price depends on how far ahead the date is: the further ahead, the cheaper, with the cheapest dates 4 months ahead. See [PFS prices](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-prices).

## VQA (TQUK)

- Online at home with an **automated invigilator**: the exam is recorded and reviewed afterwards.
- It runs in a secure web browser. The exams team sends full guidance on registration and is happy to help.
- Results take **1 to 6 working days**, the fastest option, with a digital certificate.
- There are only **3 exam papers in a cycle** before the next standardisation. Someone who has sat 3 times and still needs a resit waits for the next cycle or changes board.

**Maths:** one 2-hour exam in two sections, calculator and non-calculator, with no time limit for each. One plain sheet of paper is allowed, and the on-screen notes box is not marked. There's no own calculator: one is on screen, with a protractor and ruler. The pass mark is around 50 to 60%.

**English:** three components: reading, writing and SLC (speaking, listening and communication). Reading and writing are one 2-hour sitting (1 hour each). The SLC is on a different day and takes about 1 hour. A dictionary is allowed for reading only, there's no paper, and the on-screen notes box is not marked. The pass mark is around 55% for reading and writing, and **100% (10 out of 10) for the SLC**.

## Open Awards

- Online at home with a **human invigilator** throughout.
- It runs in a secure web browser, with full guidance from the exams team on registration.
- Results take **6 to 16 working days** after each component, with a digital certificate.
- Access arrangements take 10 working days to approve, and usually everything can be put in place: reader, scribe, rest breaks, extra time and so on.

**Maths:** one 2-hour exam (30 minutes without a calculator, 90 minutes with one). Pen and paper can be used for working, but anything to be marked must go on screen. Their own calculator, or the on-screen one. The pass mark is typically 50 to 60%.

**English:** the same three components and timings as VQA (TQUK). A dictionary is allowed for reading only: a standard Oxford dictionary, not a thesaurus or bilingual one. The pass mark is around 55% for reading and writing, and 100% (10 out of 10) for the SLC.

## The SLC (speaking, listening and communication)

- The learner joins a call with **up to 4 other learners**.
- An email arrives **7 days before** with a discussion topic, which can't be chosen.
- They prepare a **4 to 7 minute presentation**. With VQA (TQUK) they can pick their own presentation topic, which needs doing 7 days before. On the day, each learner presents.
- They're asked questions by the other learners, and must ask the others questions too.

## City and Guilds

We advise against City and Guilds:

- The learner has to book and pass a **tech test** first, then wait at least 5 working days before the exam.
- They have to download software, which slows everything down.
- Results take **up to 32 working days**.
- The SLC email comes 7 days before. There are 3 tasks: a presentation on a familiar topic (their own choice, signed off by admin), a discussion on an unfamiliar topic (three options, with the one used chosen on the day), and a discussion about a short video.
