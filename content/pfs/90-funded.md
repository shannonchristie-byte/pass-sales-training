For someone who qualifies, we pay for the **course** part. The learner pays for the **exam** only.

## Who can have one

Offer a funded course when someone has applied for it, qualifies, and has asked for it specifically or come off the funded list. They need **one or more** of:

- Not working, or working part-time
- On Universal Credit
- Dependants: for example children, or elderly or sick relatives

**Only use these as a last resort**, if the person won't sign up without a funded course:

- They have a long time to study
- It's needed to get them over the line

Offer a discount first and see how they respond. For someone in a rush who doesn't meet the criteria, only offer a funded course if they ask for it and it's needed to make the sale. It's your judgement whether someone qualifies.

If they don't qualify, reassure them we'll help them get their qualification either way, and help however we can, for example with a discount on the bundle.

## What it costs the learner

- The course is free, worth about £284 per subject.
- The exam on book now, choose the date later **must be paid and can't be discounted**. The amount is on [PFS prices](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-prices).
- Why they pay for the exam: each person has to be entered with the exam board as an individual candidate. That's how they gain the Ofqual-regulated qualification.
- They can use a payment plan, or the deposit route for exam board registration. At least the deposit must be paid before the course starts.
- **There's no free resit** with a funded course. Don't bring this up unless the learner asks.

## Putting a funded booking through

1. Add the **basic bundle** to the basket.
2. The exam must be on **book now, choose the date later**.
3. At checkout, enter the promo code: **fsfunded1!** for one subject, **fsfunded2!** for two.

## "Is there a completely free option?"

The only fully free route is a local college. It's never going to be the best option, so explain why doing it online with us is much better:

| PFS funded course | The "free" college route |
| --- | --- |
| Can start today | Can't start until September, which doesn't work for most deadlines |
| Much higher pass rates (93% when our course is completed) | Takes a full year |
| The course is individual to them | No individual focus in a class |
| As quick or slow as they like, with 24/7 access | Going into college around 2 days a week at set times |
| Sit the exam whenever they're ready, with quick results | A test centre for the exam, and a longer wait for results |
| | Travel over the year means it ends up costing about the same |
