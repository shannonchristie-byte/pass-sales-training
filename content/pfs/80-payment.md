We take **debit cards, credit cards and American Express**.

## In order of preference

| Option | How it works | Interest | Exam and results |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Pay in full**: always preferred | One payment, nothing more to pay | None | No restrictions |
| **Klarna** | 3 equal payments: on the day they register, then 1 month later, then 1 month after that | Interest free | No restrictions |
| **Clearpay** | 4 equal payments, every 2 weeks, the first on the day they register | Interest free | No restrictions |
| **PayPal** | 3 equal monthly payments, the first on the day they register | Interest free | No restrictions |
| **Payl8r** | Finance: a smaller deposit, then spread over 6, 9, 12 or 24 months. Paid through a payment link | Yes. It depends on the person, so we can't say what it will be | No restrictions |

Get as much paid upfront as possible. Only offer the deposit route once they've made it clear they can't pay in full. See the sales rules.

## Payl8r: what we can and can't say

Payl8r is credit, so the rules are strict.

**We cannot:**

- Promote or advise spreading the cost with Payl8r, for example "you could spread the cost" or "it would only be £xx"
- Recommend that a learner takes finance
- Try to access credit for them, or fill in the application
- Suggest an application will be approved

**We must:**

- Only say it's an option: "We're partnered with Payl8r" or "Payl8r are a finance provider"
- Tell them they can find out more, and see the cost breakdown, with the calculator on our website
- Send the [information page and calculator](https://payl8r.com/retailers/payment-detail?retailer=completet954ge70jote) and let them decide

## Taking payment without a call

If a learner isn't comfortable paying over the phone, set everything up your end, then send a payment link:

- Use the [PFS magic link generator](https://passfunctionalskills.co.uk/magic-login-link-generator/) with their email. They click "pay for order", and you check WooCommerce for the payment. Links last 30 minutes and 5 clicks.
- Step by step: [how to place an order for a customer](https://ct-internal.helpscoutdocs.com/article/254-how-to-place-an-order-for-a-customer-via-wordpress-front-end).
