A demo of the course and exam is a great way to show what we provide. The longer you engage with a learner, the more likely they are to register, and a demo gives you 30 minutes to talk about the benefits.

Because the learner sees everything the course offers, most people should be happy to register at the end of the call. There shouldn't be anything left for them to think about.

## Before the demo

Call **10 minutes before** to check they still want to go ahead, and ask which subject they're interested in. Tell them you'll send a Google Meet link shortly and you'll speak to them there.

## The demo, step by step

1. **Introduce yourself** and ask how they are. Keep it a natural conversation.
2. **Build rapport** as on a normal call: which qualification they need, what for, and how quickly.
3. **Reassure them** we can help them get their qualification in the time they need.
4. **Share your screen and show the course.** Circles is a good maths topic to use.
    - **Initial Assessment:** a diagnostic that finds their current level and suggests a course. It can be forced, so nothing else opens until it's done.
    - **Subject Knowledge Assessment:** another diagnostic that produces a list of recommended topics. Show how each question is marked with a written and video solution.
    - **Recommended topics:** generated by the Subject Knowledge Assessment. They're in the recommended topics bar and highlighted in purple in the course. They're marked as in progress or completed. Learners can work through the whole course or focus on these.
5. **Course content.** The Functional Skills specification is the same for every exam board. Topics are split into subtopics, each with revision, practice questions and topic tests.
    - **Revision:** videos made by a real tutor, getting more in depth as they go. Speed and captions can be adjusted, and they can be watched any number of times, 24/7.
    - **Practice:** recall questions at three difficulties. Learners pick a level and can change it any time. Multiple choice, with written and video solutions whether they're right or wrong.
    - **Topic tests:** multi-part, scenario questions like the exam, with written answers. Auto-marked with written and video solutions.
6. **Mock exams.**
    - **Full mock exams:** exam-board specific and AI marked, in practice mode (untimed, marked question by question) or exam mode (timed, marked at the end).
    - **Practice exams:** more general extra practice, in either mode.
    - **Past papers:** links to the boards' real past papers. The Open Awards one opens in the XAMS software, exactly as on exam day. These aren't marked and answers aren't saved.
    - **English AI marking:** to show it, use Open Awards English mock 1 and enter this model answer: "The first stage of the cloning process is to remove the nucleus from the somatic cell. Cloning may not be effective as cloned cells need to be taken from undamaged cells which will cause issues for cloning extinct species."
7. **The dashboard.** A live view of progress: course completion (and whether they're on track for a planned end date), average scores on practice questions and topic tests, and their current level.
8. **Other points:** 24/7 access, as quick or slow as they like, around 50 hours of study recommended, and a 93% success rate when completed.
9. **Questions?** Ask if they have any questions about the course. If not, stop sharing the course and **share the exam screen**.
10. **The exam.** Explain how they'll get the exam link and the invigilation. Show how to move between questions: they can go back and forth freely and use pen and paper for notes. For maths, they can use words instead of numbers if that's easier.
11. **Results:** for maths, 1 to 6 working days with VQA (TQUK), a digital certificate, and the qualification is Ofqual regulated.
12. **Make the booking.** Open a new incognito tab and talk them through it as normal.

> **To check:** step 10 says exams are marked by humans, and step 6 says the demo has 20 exam-board-specific maths mocks. Both differ from [The courses](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-courses).
