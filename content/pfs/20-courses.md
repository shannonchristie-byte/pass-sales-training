Online maths and English courses, open 24/7 straight after registration. There are interactive demos to share: [maths demo](https://app.supademo.com/demo/cmd37vg3i3kl9c4kjjvwlgdl3?step=2) and [English demo](https://app.supademo.com/demo/cmdihed1n02akyq0i2oe75p47).

## Highlights

- We recommend **around 50 hours of study**, at the learner's own pace. It's a guide, not a requirement.
- A **complete course**, with no need for other revision materials or tutoring.
- A **93% success rate** when the course is completed.
- **Access lasts 12 months** with the premium bundle or course only, and **3 months** with the basic bundle.

## How the course works

1. **Initial Assessment.** A 30-minute mini test that shows the learner's current level and which level we recommend. It's only a guide.
2. **Subject Knowledge Assessment.** A mini test with a question on each topic. It creates a **Personal Learning Plan**: the topics to focus on, based on their results.
3. **Course material.** Every topic that could come up in the exam is covered, in any order. Each topic has three parts:
    - **Revise:** written slides and videos of an expert tutor explaining the key terms, the knowledge and the best method for each question type. They can be watched as often as needed.
    - **Practice questions:** quick-fire multiple choice, at easy, medium and hard. Each answer is marked, with the correct answer, a written solution and a video solution.
    - **Topic tests:** questions like the real exam, typed rather than multiple choice. They're marked the same way and can be retaken as often as needed.
4. **Mock exams.** These match the question types and structure of the chosen exam board. They're AI marked by Dr Clive with instant feedback: marks gained and lost, and model answers, even for essay-style English questions.
    - **Practice mode:** no time limit, marked question by question.
    - **Exam mode:** timed like the real exam and marked only at the end. This is the closest thing to the real exam.

## The dashboard and "exam ready"

The dashboard shows the learner how they're doing at any time:

- **Course progress:** how far through the course they are. Useful for checking they're on track for a deadline.
- **Current level:** starts at the initial level and moves with their results.
- **Revision time required:** the average time students take to get from their current level to exam ready.
- **Average scores** on practice questions and mocks. Aim for above 60%, as exams typically need 55 to 60% to pass.

**Exam ready** means the learner is prepared to sit the exam. They've:

1. Completed the Subject Knowledge Assessment
2. Completed the Personal Learning Plan
3. Completed practice papers
4. Completed mock exams
5. Reached a current level above 1.5 (Level 1 courses) or 2.5 (Level 2 courses)

> **To check:** the demo guide says to recommend the exam once averages are above 65%; this section says 60%.

## Accessibility

The accessibility feature changes how the course looks so it works for as many learners as possible. For example: font size, screen colour, hiding images, a reading guide and read-aloud text to speech.

## Entry Level 3

We also offer Entry Level 3 maths and English, below Level 1. Only offer it if someone asks for it specifically. The courses work the same way as the Functional Skills courses, and so does the exam set-up.

- Entry Level 3 exams are with **Open Awards only**.
- **English:** reading 45 minutes and writing 50 minutes.
- **Maths:** 30 minutes without a calculator and 1 hour 15 minutes with one.

> **To check:** the doc says the Level 1 and Level 2 courses are also on a subscription plan "not something that D2L team can sell". Confirm whether sales should mention it at all.

## Digital Skills Level 1

A course only, with no exam. It builds practical digital skills for work, study and everyday life: using technology confidently and staying safe online. It works like the maths and English courses:

1. A Subject Knowledge Assessment
2. Revise (interactive walkthroughs and slides), practice questions (multiple choice) and topic tests (written answers, AI marked with feedback and a model answer)
3. One full mock paper, multiple choice and written, marked by Dr Clive
4. Progress tracking: course progress, current level and scores. There's no "exam ready" for Digital Skills.
