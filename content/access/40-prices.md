## Payment options

We take debit cards, credit cards and American Express. Pay in full is always preferred. The options are **Klarna, Clearpay, PayPal and Payl8r**. They work the same as for PFS: see [PFS payment options](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-payment).

**Payl8r is finance.** Only say it's an option, and point them to the [calculator](https://payl8r.com/retailers/payment-detail?retailer=completet954ge70jote). Never recommend it, quote monthly amounts, help them apply or suggest they'll be approved.

If they ask about spreading the cost, say: "We do have payment options available, including Klarna, Clearpay, PayPal and Payl8r. The options available depend on the payment method and the provider's checks."

To take payment without a call, use the [Pass Learn magic link generator](https://passlearn.co.uk/magic-login-link-generator/).

## Refunds

- **Application rejected:** they're entitled to a refund.
- When they apply, the learner picks a **start date**. That's when they're registered with Open Awards and can start submitting. **After the start date, no refund is due**, because we can't withdraw them and we've paid the fees.
