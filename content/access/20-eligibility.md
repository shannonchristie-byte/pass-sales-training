Before completing a sale, check three things: is the learner **eligible**, **compatible** and **suitable**?

## 1. Eligible: must be true to go ahead

- Are they **16 or over**?
- Are they a **UK resident**?
- Do they have **Level 2 maths and English** (GCSE or Functional Skills), with evidence? If not, they can start and work towards them alongside the diploma, but they must pass them to complete it. **We're one of the only providers that allows this**, and we can help with Functional Skills.
- Do they have **internet access and a laptop or computer** for the course and assignments?
- Have they **attempted a diploma before**, with us or anyone else? Ask it as "getting the context of your previous qualifications". People sometimes leave this out, which causes problems later with recognising prior learning and awarding credits.

**Anyone not eligible can't go ahead.**

## 2. Compatible: will it get them where they want to go?

- What do they want the diploma for? University? What are their aspirations?
- Which degree and university? Does that university accept Access to HE for that course? Flag it if you're not sure.

If their long-term goal is something the diploma definitely can't support, **don't advise them to buy**. Where it's unclear, the support team researches it first.

## 3. Suitable: can they realistically do it?

- What time can they commit each week?
- How motivated are they?
- Do they understand how hard it is, and are they willing to work hard?
- Do they have any access or support needs, and are they adjustments we can make?

People who may not be entirely suitable can still buy. Suitability is assessed again in the application after purchase. If they're rejected, the refund policy applies.

**Watch for signs someone isn't ready or suitable** during the call. Set realistic expectations without underselling: be honest about the effort, but reassure them it's achievable.

## Information to capture on every call

- Name, and the diploma they want
- Their intended university course or career, and target universities if known
- Whether they've checked entry requirements
- Whether they have Level 2 maths and English, or may need Functional Skills or GCSEs alongside
- Whether they've studied Access to HE before
- Age and UK residency confirmed
- Computer and internet access
- Study time available each week
- Motivation and confidence
- Any access or support needs
- That they understand: university entry isn't guaranteed, there's an application after purchase, and how hard and how much work it is

## What happens after they buy

1. **Application.** The buyer gets a confirmation email and a link to the application form, filled in by the applicant or someone on their behalf.
2. **Admin checks:** ID, ULN (unique learner number), the reasonable adjustments we can offer, suitability, then the approval decision.
    - **Rejected:** they're entitled to a refund.
    - **Approved:** we ask for evidence of prior qualifications and a start date, then enrol them with Pass Learn and they can access the learning materials.
3. **Registration** with Open Awards, through the Open Awards portal, on the start date they picked.
4. **Once registered with Open Awards, there's no refund.** This is the point of no return. From then on, their outcome (completed or withdrawn) is reported to Open Awards. They get their logins and manage their diploma from there.

## Reasonable adjustments

There are **no exams**, so the usual arrangements (extra time, a reader) don't apply. We can adapt how the course is presented, for example with accessibility features, and apply for **deadline extensions** on assignments. **The assignments themselves can't change.**
