**This is the one place for Pass GCSE prices.** Always check the product page on [passmygcse.co.uk](https://passmygcse.co.uk/) before quoting.

## Prices, per subject

| Product | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Exam | From £351.20. Late booking fees are added the later they book: up to £499 or more per exam |
| Course | £199.20 |
| Course and exam bundle: Maths, English or Biology | £451.20 |
| Course and exam bundle: Combined Science, single award | £547.20 |
| Course and exam bundle: Combined Science, double award | £100 more than single award |
| English speaking component | £100 on top of the exam |
| Access arrangements | Charged by the centre: usually £100 to £150 per arrangement |

## Discounts

The website's sales plug-in shows a limited-time offer, which helps with urgency.

- **No discount on exam-only bookings at all. No exceptions.**
- The only discount is **10% off the course**, for course only or the course in a bundle. Only offer it if the learner asks, or you'll lose the sale at the current price.

| Code | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| management10! | 10% off the course part only |
| 99course! | Makes the course £99 |
| 49course! | Makes the course £49 |

A discount costs stars as well as revenue. See [What a sale is worth](https://b2c-sales.onrender.com/#/bonus/star-values).

## Payment options

- Pay in full (always preferred), **Klarna, Clearpay or PayPal**. They work the same as for PFS: see [PFS payment options](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-payment).
- **Payl8r** is finance. We must never be seen to advise it. Follow the Payl8r rules on the PFS payment page.
