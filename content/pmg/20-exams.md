Exams are sat **in person at an exam centre** near the learner, on set dates: **May** and **November** (resits).

## What happens after booking

1. The learner reserves an exam place by booking on our website, where we take payment.
2. An order confirmation is emailed straight away.
3. The Pass GCSE exams team emails more information and guidance. **The learner must reply with all their details, or they can't be booked into a centre.** Make sure they know this.
4. We book them into the nearest centre that offers their exam board. That's usually 30 to 60 minutes' drive, but up to 2 hours if they book late. Admin emails to check they're happy, and they must confirm or decline **within 1 week** (they get a reminder if not). Once booked, the centre can't be changed without a fee.
5. We confirm the time, place and details well before exam day. **Once that's confirmed by email, nothing can change**: not the exams, the exam board, access arrangements or location.

The booking price is for the exam only. **It doesn't include access arrangement fees**, which are invoiced later. See [Access arrangements and results](#/kb/p?slug=pmg-access-results).

## Flexible exam series

A booking stays valid up to and including the equivalent exam series **two years later**. For example, a booking made before the end of the summer 2026 series can be used for any eligible series up to and including summer 2028.

- The learner must contact us to confirm which series they'll sit, and it must be available for their subject.
- Late-entry fees may apply, depending on when they confirm. We tell them before the entry is processed.
- **Once their series is chosen and confirmed by admin, no refund is due.** If they don't sit by the last eligible series, the booking expires.
- Not available in November: Combined Science single award; English Literature, History and Business with AQA or Edexcel; English speaking (except Edexcel IGCSE where it applies). Maths and English Language in November are for resits or Edexcel IGCSE only.

## Maths

[Dates for each exam board](https://passmygcse.co.uk/shop/exams/gcse-maths-resit/)

- **3 papers**, each 1 hour 30 minutes, on different dates: paper 1 non-calculator, papers 2 and 3 calculator. All three must be sat. They don't have to pass each one: strong results on two can make up for a weak third.
- **Foundation** for a grade 4 or 5 (old grade C). **Higher** for grade 6 and above.
- Exam boards: AQA, Edexcel, OCR, WJEC and Edexcel IGCSE. **If they don't know, use Edexcel**, the most common.

## English

[Dates for each exam board](https://passmygcse.co.uk/shop/exams/gcse-english-resit/)

- **2 papers**, English Language papers 1 and 2, each 1 hour 45 minutes. There are no tiers: everyone sits the same exam, for grade 4 up to 9.
- Exam boards: AQA, Edexcel and OCR. **If they don't know, use AQA.**
- **Speaking component:** essential to the overall qualification, with its own certificate (pass, merit or distinction). It's an extra charge (see [prices](#/kb/p?slug=pmg-prices)). Anyone who has done the speaking before doesn't need to redo it. Anyone who hasn't must add it. It's at their chosen centre, on a date arranged between them, and **can't be done in November**.

## Science, Biology, Chemistry and Physics

All are based on the **Edexcel IGCSE** specification, which has **no practical element**, because centres can't run practicals. Anyone who has already done the practicals can sit another board, for example AQA.
