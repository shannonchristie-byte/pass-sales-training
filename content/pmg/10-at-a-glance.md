Pass GCSE (PMG) offers **the full GCSE qualification**, the same as is sat in schools. Exams are on **set dates each year**: everyone sits on the same day, and results come out nationally on the same day.

**Still advise Functional Skills to anyone it suits.** Only go for GCSE when Functional Skills isn't right for them. See [Functional Skills or GCSE?](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-at-a-glance).

## What we offer

- **Exams** in a wide range of subjects, sat in person at an exam centre near the learner. See [Exams and booking](#/kb/p?slug=pmg-exams).
- **Courses** in Maths (foundation and higher), English Language (including IGCSE), Combined Science (single and double award), Biology and Chemistry.
- **Course and exam bundles.** Prices are on [Pass GCSE prices and discounts](#/kb/p?slug=pmg-prices).

## The courses

- Online, at the learner's own pace, with **12 months' access**.
- We recommend **around 100 hours of study per subject**.
- There's **no Initial Assessment or Subject Knowledge Assessment** on these courses yet.
- Each topic has three parts:
    - **Revise:** videos or revision slides covering the knowledge and how to apply it to exam questions.
    - **Practice questions:** multiple choice at easy, medium and hard. Each answer is marked, with the correct answer and a written solution.
    - **Online tests:** typed answers, closer to the real exam, marked with written and video solutions.
- **Mock exams** at the end, for each exam board.
- Progress is saved throughout and shown on the dashboard.

## Can't get to an exam centre?

Pearson Edexcel International GCSEs can be sat online from home. See [Online exams at a glance](#/kb/p?slug=exams-at-a-glance). If the learner can get to a centre, the centre exam is cheaper.
