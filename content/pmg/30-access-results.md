## Access arrangements

**Ask on every call whether they need access arrangements.** Explain how it works, and stress that they must send evidence and reply to our emails after booking.

- They must send evidence **within a day of booking**, so we can start the application and find a centre that can accommodate them.
- There's a **cut-off** for each series. After it, nothing can be arranged in time, because it's out of our control. They can sit without arrangements, or move to a later series.
- **Each centre does its own in-house assessment**, whatever evidence they already have (previous assessments, doctor's notes, diagnostic letters).
- Most centres need a **JCQ Form 8**, filled in by a SENCo (special educational needs coordinator) or a specialist assessor at a school or college.
- **The exam price doesn't include access arrangement fees.** These are charged by the centre, not us, usually **£100 to £150 per arrangement**. Admin invoices once a suitable centre has been found and the fee is known. We can show them the evidence of the charge.

## Results

- **GCSE results day is in August.**
- The centre explains how results arrive: an email from us, direct contact from the centre, or collecting them at the centre. If collecting isn't practical, they can ask for them to be emailed.
- **Certificates are paper only** and can take a long time. The statement of results they get is valid proof until the certificate arrives.
- We can also write a **provider letter** confirming their result.
