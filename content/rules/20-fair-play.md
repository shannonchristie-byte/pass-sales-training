We're one team working for the biggest team total, because the team targets make the biggest difference to everyone's bonus. Treat other people and their stars the way you'd want to be treated if it were the other way round.

| Situation | Who gets the stars |
| --- | --- |
| You're off sick | Your leads are called while you're off. Whoever makes the sale gets the stars |
| You're on holiday | Your leads are shared out. Whoever converts gets the stars. You can pick up others' leads when they're on holiday, so it evens out |
| An invoice you sent is paid while you're away | Still your stars |
| A learner says they've spoken to someone else | Ask for the name. If they can name them, pass it over. If not, it's your sale |
| Two people have both spoken to a learner, or someone took a call for you | Agree a split, as long as it's fair |
| An email thread has someone's name on it | It's their lead: don't reply yourself. If they're off sick or on holiday, reply for them but keep their name at the bottom. If the learner then asks for a call to buy, whoever calls can take it |
| Funded emails and similar | Anyone can pick these up |

## If you think someone has broken the rules

Handle it professionally, assuming an innocent mistake. **Slack Shannon discreetly** so it can be looked into. Don't talk about it openly in the office, and stay positive where you can.
