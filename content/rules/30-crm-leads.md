All call leads and callbacks for every service are in the **Sales CRM** (Airtable). There are general leads anyone can take, and your own personal callbacks. Full guide: [how to use the Sales CRM](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1wGS3Jwnyj3_zOrhlCOhVJlJor7EN0JGmpROPcudwv6Q/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.m29wwoxoy7oy).

## General leads

- Start on **Hot Leads** (enquiry forms, Facebook leads and other priority leads), sorted by **Dr Clive rating**, with 5 the best.
- **Learning Platforms** is learners who registered for the free version of the learning platform but haven't bought anything.
- Anyone trained on a lead type can call it. **Mark that you're calling before you call**, because everyone works from the same lists.
- Once you mark a call, the lead disappears until it's due again. If you can see it, you can call it.
- If you set a callback and add your name, it moves to your personal callbacks.
- What each lead type is, and its script: [Lead types](#/kb/p?slug=scripts-lead-types).

## How many calls

- **Facebook leads: 1 call**, then dead lead (the automatic email still goes).
- **Other leads: 3 calls**, at different times of day. Leave a voicemail each time and mark the call. Leave a decent gap: if you called in the morning, wait until after 2pm; if you called in the afternoon, wait until after 5pm or the next day.
- On the third unanswered call, add your name to show it was made, and send the follow-up email.
- On your own callbacks, where you know they need to buy, chase as you judge best, without pestering.

## Your personal callbacks

Leads you've spoken to and arranged a callback with. **Only call leads in your own sections**, unless management or that person asks you to.

| Section | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| Today | Callbacks due today |
| Tomorrow | Tomorrow's schedule |
| The Past | Overdue callbacks. **Keep this empty**: check it every day |
| All Leads | Everything: overdue, today, tomorrow and later |

**Adding a lead** (after an inbound call or emails): click **Add Lead**, then fill in name, email, number, the website they're buying through, and a message with what they need. Set the date and time and add your name. Use a **chill callback** for a general morning or afternoon, or a **specific callback** for an agreed time.

**On each call:**

- Click the learner's name to open their details and your message.
- Every call, use **Mark Call & Arrange Call Back**. If they don't answer, pick when to try again.
- If you speak but don't sell, use **Reschedule Call-Back** with the agreed date and time.
- Add notes as you go. Notes go in the **notes field**, not Airtable comments, because comments don't sync between bases.
- Follow-up email: pick from the **Email Template Selector**. Always put your name in **Sender Name**, and pick the template for the right company, or it won't send.

**A sale:** add the stars and the total paid (the full amount, even on a payment plan), then **Confirm Sale**. The lead leaves your callbacks. To record the sale itself, use the sale form on the Sales CRM interface.

**No sale and no more calls needed:** **dead lead** it with an accurate reason, because the reason decides what happens to the lead next. Only tick **Do not send emails** if it's really necessary, for example someone angry or complaining. Then **Confirm Dead Lead**.

## Before calling some lead types

Check WooCommerce first, so you don't call someone who has already bought. See [Lead types](#/kb/p?slug=scripts-lead-types) for which checks each type needs.
