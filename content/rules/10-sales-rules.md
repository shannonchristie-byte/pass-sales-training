## The 6 sales rules

1. **Get the info.** Don't end a call without the key information needed to fill in the callback details on the CRM.
2. **Costs.** Avoid talking about cost until you've built a rapport. Reassure them our pricing is the lowest available.
3. **Urgency.** Find a way to make it vital they book today, using what they told you while building rapport.
4. **Pay in full.** Get as much paid upfront as possible, even on the deposit route. Never offer the deposit route until they've made it clear they can't pay in full.
5. **Discounts.** Only offer one if you need to, to make the sale.
6. **Book now.** An order is never guaranteed until it's paid. Booking now always beats booking next week, even if it means a deposit now instead of full payment next week.

How to find or create urgency, and how to offer discounts: [Urgency and discounts](#/kb/p?slug=objection-urgency-discounts).
