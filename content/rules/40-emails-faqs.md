## Non-sale calls

Lots of people call about things they can find on the website, get by email, do in their online account, or need to email about. **Get these calls finished quickly.**

If admin could sort it quickly on the phone, ask the learner to call back and choose the **existing student option: option 2** on the PFS, A Star and Pass GCSE lines.

| They want | What to tell them |
| --- | --- |
| **A refund** | It must be requested by email, not on the phone, so there's a paper trail for us and for them. Never promise a refund |
| **To book a date for a prepaid exam** | They can do it in their online account. If they have trouble, email the exams team |
| **To change an exam date** | Request it in their online account, call back on the admin option, or email in |
| **A remark** | Email the exams team. We generally don't advise it: markers already check, and the mark could go down. Scripts near a grade boundary are always checked for extra marks |
| **Access arrangements** | Send evidence to the exams team for their service as soon as they've booked. It can take a while: see each service's exam page |
