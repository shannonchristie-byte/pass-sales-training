## Coursework

**Subjects:** English Language, English Literature, English Language and Literature, History, Computer Science and Geography. We can't offer Geography fieldwork yet, so Geography is exam only.

When coursework is paid for, the booking covers everything needed to prepare, complete and mark it, with final grades. It's usually one or more written pieces completed over a set time.

## Practicals

**Subjects:** Biology, Chemistry and Physics.

- The **practical endorsement** is reported separately from the exam grade, as pass or fail. It shows the learner has the required practical skills.
- **A learner can get the A Level without it, but almost all universities require it for science degrees.** Make sure they understand this before choosing exam only.
- Anyone who has passed the endorsement before doesn't need to retake it for a resit, as long as they have the evidence. Anyone sitting for the first time, or who didn't pass it, should take it.
- We can arrange the practical endorsement **for 2027 onwards**: the practical activities, assessment, guidance and support. Practicals happen on separate dates in a lab near the exam centre, arranged as part of the booking.
- The endorsement carries across exam boards, because AQA, Edexcel and OCR all assess it against the same Common Practical Assessment Criteria (CPAC).

## Carrying forward previous coursework or practicals

Previous results can usually be carried forward for the lifetime of the specification, if they meet the exam board's requirements and are submitted in time. The learner needs:

- Their statement of results
- Their certificate
- The NEA authentication sheet

## Access arrangements

- Evidence is required: a **JCQ Form 8 or 9 (mandatory)**, a normal way of working form, or an EHCP.
- Fees vary between centres, so there's no set fee we can quote. The exams team works through the options, pricing and evidence with the learner.
- **The deadline for summer exams: booking and all evidence in before 20 December the year before.**

## UCAS points

Each subject page shows the UCAS points for each grade. They're the same whatever the exam board or centre.

| Grade | UCAS points |
| --- | --- |
| A* | 56 |
| A | 48 |
| B | 40 |
| C | 32 |
| D | 24 |
| E | 16 |

Cambridge International A Levels don't carry UCAS points, but universities treat them the same. See [Subjects, exams and booking](#/kb/p?slug=alevel-exams).

## UCAS predicted grades and academic reference

We provide UCAS predicted grades and the academic reference for A Levels.

- Only for learners **who have bought the course**.
- An add-on (see [A Level prices](#/kb/p?slug=alevel-prices)).
- Based on their course progress, if they've done enough, and/or a timed assessment we run.
- Provided by January, before the university application deadline, or sooner.
