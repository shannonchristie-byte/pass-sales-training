## Sitting A Levels as a private candidate

The only free way to sit an A Level is to be enrolled at a school or college that registers you for the exams. The alternative is to enter as a **private candidate** through us, which has a cost.

The benefit is that the learner controls the schedule. At college, A Levels take 2 years. With online learning and a private booking, they can revise as fast as they like for the summer exams. **Someone could book in January, sit the exams in May or June, and start university that September.**

There are **no official entry requirements**. A GCSE grade C/4 in a related subject helps but isn't essential. They can book a first attempt or a resit.

## Is Access to HE a better fit?

Not everyone needs A Levels to get into university. If the caller's real goal is a degree rather than the A Levels themselves, raise the [Access to HE Diploma](#/kb/p?slug=access-at-a-glance).

**Suggest Access to HE when:**

- They're an adult whose goal is a specific degree
- They want a faster route (one year instead of two)
- They're anxious about exams or have struggled with exam-based study
- They're returning to education without recent qualifications

**A Levels are still better when:**

- They're under 16 (Access courses are for adults)
- Their target university or course requires A Levels (for example, medicine at some universities)
- They want the most flexibility to change degree plans later
- They might apply to universities outside the UK
