A Level exams are **only in the summer**, from mid-May to late June, sat **in person at a local exam centre**. We have centres across the country, and the sooner they book, the closer to home it's likely to be. Cambridge International exams also run in a November series.

Learners outside the UK can book with us if they can travel to the UK for the exams. Some Cambridge International A Levels can be sat at approved centres abroad: refer these to the exams team.

Each subject page on the website has the specifics and dates. Exam boards cover similar content for the same subject, but syllabuses and exam methods differ.

## Cambridge International A Levels

We also offer Cambridge International A Levels in the **November series**. Dates are at the bottom of each subject page, and run from late September to November despite the name.

- Only Cambridge International runs outside the summer. There are no age or attempt restrictions, and none of the subjects we offer has practicals or coursework.
- **There's no disadvantage to the learner.** They don't carry UCAS tariff points, but most universities (including the Russell Group and Cambridge) make grade-based offers, such as AAB. Universities treat them as interchangeable with UK A Levels grade for grade. The few that use UCAS points treat them identically too.
- **On UCAS:** in the education section, search for "Cambridge International A Level" instead of the standard UK A Level, then add the subject and grade as normal.

## How booking works

1. **Book the exam place.** Confirm subjects and exam boards. For practical or coursework subjects, decide whether it's exam only or whether we also arrange the practical or coursework (extra cost).
2. **Registration.** The exams team contacts the nearest centres that offer their subjects and boards, offers one, and secures it once the learner confirms. They ask for anything else they need.
3. **Revise**, using our courses.
4. **Exams.** The exams team confirms the time, place and details well in advance.
5. **Results day.** All results come out on the same day, usually from the centre directly. Some centres ask learners to collect them. Anyone applying to university should have their UCAS login ready.
6. **Next steps:** university, a job, or a resit.

**On a resit, the highest grade stands.** A lower grade never overrides a higher one, even if it's more recent.
