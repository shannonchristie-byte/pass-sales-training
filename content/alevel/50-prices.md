**This is the one place for A Level prices.** Prices vary by subject, so always check the subject page on [passlearn.co.uk](https://passlearn.co.uk/online-a-levels/exams/) before quoting.

**Late fees** are added around the end of January and again at the end of February, and they increase the price dramatically. Strongly recommend booking before then.

## Prices, per subject

These are current prices after the 20% sales plug-in, before any late fees, and not including practicals or coursework.

| Product | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Exam only: Maths, Sciences, Psychology, Sociology | £527.20 |
| Exam only: English Language, English Literature, English Language and Literature, Computer Science | £679.20 |
| Exam only: Business Studies, Economics | £487.20 |

## Payment options

| Option | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Pay in full** (preferred) | One payment |
| **Klarna** | 3 equal interest-free monthly payments, the first on the day they register |
| **Clearpay** | 4 equal interest-free payments every 2 weeks, the first on the day they register |
| **PayPal** | 3 equal interest-free monthly payments, the first on the day they register |
| **[Partial.ly](http://partial.ly)** | A deposit, then the rest split over up to 6 months, interest free. Minimum spend £999. **Stars are halved** with Partial.ly |

Try to take payment in full without mentioning payment plans, unless they ask or seem worried about paying.
