## How the course works

1. **Initial Assessment**, then a **Subject Knowledge Assessment** that builds a Personal Learning Plan.
2. **Every topic of the syllabus**, each in three parts:
    - **Revise:** revision slides with audio, covering the knowledge for the topic and how to apply it to exam questions.
    - **Practice questions:** multiple choice at easy, medium and hard, marked instantly with the correct answer and a written solution.
    - **Topic tests:** typed answers, closer to the real exam, AI marked with written and video solutions.
3. **Mock exams** at the end, tailored to each exam board.
4. **Progress tracking**, saved throughout and shown on the dashboard.

Learners using our course are more likely to pass first time, and it helps them brush up on areas they were weaker in last time.
