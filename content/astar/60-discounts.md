## The rules

- **Only offer a discount if the learner asks for one, or you'll lose the sale at the current price.**
- **No discounts on exams.** Discounts only apply to course only, or the course part of a bundle.
- Course and exam booked together: up to **10% off the course part**.
- **Fast track** can be discounted to £49 when needed.
- **Three subjects** (courses and exams): if needed, one of the courses can be free.
- A discount costs stars as well as revenue. See [What a sale is worth](https://b2c-sales.onrender.com/#/bonus/star-values).

## How to offer one

- Make the learner feel special. Make it clear the discount applies to them for a reason, for example a blue light discount.
- Make sure the discount is **only for today**. If they choose a callback, say you can put the request in but can't promise it will be granted. Try to create a now-or-never situation with the discount.

## Bringing the price down without a discount

For exam only or a bundle, suggest **a date further ahead**. Exam bookings work like flights: the earlier they book, the cheaper it can be.

## The resit list

Calls to people on the A Star resit list are **more of a support call than a sales call**.

- Ask what revision they've been using. If it's their own, explain why the course is the best revision there is: what's included, and the much higher success rate when it's completed.
- Try to get them to book their resit first. If they haven't had the course before, try to sell the course too. You can offer 10% off the course when they book a resit.
- Still avoid discounting. But for resits booked from the callback list, you can offer **free fast-track results** to get them over the line if needed.
