Offer a demo to learners asking a lot of questions on Tawk, or anyone hesitant on the phone. It shows the course materials and the free extra resources, and gives time for a fuller conversation about the exam system and how the process works.

Demos use our own A Star account ([my account](https://astarequivalency.co.uk/my-account/)) and the exam system ([exam.net](https://exam.net/)). **The logins aren't kept here.** Ask Poppy for them.

## The demo, step by step

1. **Introduce yourself** and ask how they are. Keep it a natural conversation.
2. **Build rapport** with the usual questions: "Which subjects do you need?", "What deadline are you working to?", "What do you need the qualifications for?"
3. **Reassure them** that we can help them get their qualifications in time, and that we've helped hundreds of people in the same situation succeed.
4. **Share your screen** and open the product page for their subject. Show how to reach the past papers and mark schemes, and the links to the MME website for maths and English.
5. **Show the online account:** their booked exams, and how to reach the pre-recorded and live webinar videos. The join now button goes green when a live session can be joined. Use this to stress that the sooner they register, the more lessons they get.
6. **Show the exam system.** Don't share your screen until the exam you're showing is loaded. Show how questions are answered on screen and how it all works.
7. **Get them registered.** Highlight what makes registering today better than in a few days or weeks. Answer their questions and fit it around their situation.
