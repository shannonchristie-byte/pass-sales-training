**This is the one place for A Star prices.** Every other A Star page links here.

Prices change with exam dates, slot availability and sales. Always check the product page on [astarequivalency.co.uk](https://astarequivalency.co.uk/) before quoting. "BNCDL" means book now, choose the date later.

## Per subject

| Product | Price | Most you can discount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Premium bundle (BNCDL) | £488 | 10% off the course part |
| Basic bundle (BNCDL) | £388 | 10% off the course part |
| Course only (premium, 12 months) | £289 | 10%. Can be reduced to £99, or £49 as a last resort (see [What we offer](#/kb/p?slug=astar-what-we-offer)) |
| Exam only (BNCDL), any subject | £199 | No discount |
| Exam only on a chosen date, any subject | From £149 | No discount |

Choosing a date when booking a bundle brings its price down too.
