## Payment options

We take **debit cards, credit cards and American Express**.

| Option | How it works | Exam and results |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pay in full**: always preferred | One payment, nothing more to pay | No restrictions |
| **Klarna** | 3 equal interest-free payments: on the day they register, then 1 month later, then 1 month after that | No restrictions |
| **Clearpay** | 4 equal interest-free payments, every 2 weeks, the first on the day they register | No restrictions |
| **PayPal** | 3 equal interest-free monthly payments, the first on the day they register | No restrictions |

To take payment without a call, use the [A Star magic link generator](https://astarequivalency.co.uk/magic-login-link-generator/) with their email.

## Provider letters

A headed, signed letter from us confirming the exam is booked and how long results should take. It also says that passing gives them their GCSE-equivalent qualification in that subject. Letters often take the pressure off and help stop university or job offers being withdrawn. **They're free for anyone under time pressure who books with us.**

## Cancelling an exam

- There's a **14-day cooling-off period if no exam date has been chosen**.
- **Once a date has been chosen, no refund is due.**
- Cancellations involve admin or cancellation fees. Admin discusses these with the learner.

Sales never promise a refund on the phone. The learner emails in, and admin confirms or declines it.

## Changing subject

- If they've started the original course, the new course has to be bought again.
- For the exam, the admin fee depends on whether a date has been picked, and is typically £50.
