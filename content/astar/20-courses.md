Online courses the learner can complete as quickly or slowly as they like. **They're the only revision source tailored to these exams**, and learners who take the course have a much higher success rate.

- We recommend **around 90 hours of study per subject**.
- Every topic that could come up in the exam is covered, with three parts for each:
    - **Revision:** slides and comprehensive tutor-led videos covering the key points, with worked examples of exam-style questions.
    - **Practice questions:** quick-fire multiple choice to reinforce what they've learnt.
    - **Topic tests:** questions in the exam style, with full written or video solutions for every test.
- **Mock exams and past papers.**
- **AI marking** with instant feedback throughout.
- **Progress tracking:** their current level, how far through the course they are, and their average scores.

Access lasts **12 months** with the premium bundle or course only, and **3 months** with the basic bundle. See [What we offer](#/kb/p?slug=astar-what-we-offer).
