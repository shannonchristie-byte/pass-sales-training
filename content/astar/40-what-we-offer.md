Four ways to buy, in the order to offer them. Prices are on [A Star prices](#/kb/p?slug=astar-prices). **Fast-track results** (2 working days instead of 10) can be added to any of them.

## 1. Premium bundle: always offer this first

- The premium course for **12 months**
- The full exam
- **One free resit**

To bring the price down, pick an exam date when booking. Use the calendar to find the cheapest date available.

## 2. Basic bundle: the fallback

Offer it once the premium bundle has been explored. It suits someone who wants a cheaper option, or doesn't need 12 months' access or the free resit, for example someone in a rush or very confident.

- **3 months** of course access, instead of 12
- The exam
- **No free resit**

## 3. Course only

Only the full premium course, with 12 months' access. It can be reduced in certain situations, for example:

- They've already booked the exam elsewhere
- They booked the exam with us, and we're calling nearer the exam to help with revision
- Their course access has run out and they want to keep revising

## 4. Exam only

The exam with no course material. We don't usually advise it, but it suits someone confident of passing, someone who needs the exam as soon as possible, or someone who has already revised with our course. They can pick a date when booking, or use book now, choose the date later.

## Discounts

The most is **10% off the course part**, never the exam. See [Discounts, offers and resits](#/kb/p?slug=astar-discounts).
