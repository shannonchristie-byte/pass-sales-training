A Star Equivalency is the exam board. The exams are benchmarked against Ofqual-regulated exam boards for each subject.

## How the exams work

- **Online at home with a human invigilator** over Google Meet. A paper-based option is also available (below).
- On the day, the learner is emailed a Google Meet link 10 to 15 minutes before the start. They join, the invigilator talks through the instructions and shares the exam link, and the questions appear on screen.
- Exams run **7 days a week** at various times. Availability is limited because invigilation is by a person.
- The further ahead they book, the cheaper it usually is. Prices move more than PFS because they depend on the spaces left in each slot.
- They need a computer with a camera, microphone and keyboard, a stable internet connection, and a phone that can scan or photograph. No special hardware, software or training is needed.
- **Results take up to 10 working days** for every subject, or **2 working days with fast track** (an extra fee per exam, see [A Star prices](#/kb/p?slug=astar-prices)).

## The four exams

### Maths: foundation or higher?

- For a **grade 4 or 5 (grade C)**, advise **foundation**. It's a shorter course with much higher pass rates.
- **Higher** is needed for **grade 6 and above** (up to A*).
- Advise everyone to sit foundation. The one exception is **teaching in Wales** that needs a grade 6 in GCSE maths: that needs higher.

### English

English Language only, no literature, with non-fiction sources. 100 marks in total. They can't annotate the sources but can use paper for notes. No dictionary.

### Combined Science and Biology

Short-form answers, with questions of 1 to 6 marks in different formats. Combined Science is split equally across biology, physics and chemistry (a third each).

- **Combined Science** is usually best for **primary teaching**.
- **Biology** is usually best for **secondary teaching**.

## Paper-based exams

Still invigilated live over Google Meet. The paper, questions and answers are just on paper.

- The paper arrives by post about **1 week before** the exam, in a sealed envelope. Inside is a security-sealed envelope marked not to be opened until they're on the call with the invigilator.
- A **prepaid envelope** is included to send it back. It must be sealed on camera at the end, while the invigilator watches.
- They can book paper-based from the start, or upgrade an online booking for a fee. Bundles can still be bought with paper-based exams. Prices: see [A Star prices](#/kb/p?slug=astar-prices).
- **The results clock starts when the paper reaches our office**, for both standard and fast track. For example, a learner who sits on the 1st and posts on the 5th, with the paper arriving on the 7th, gets results 10 working days (or 2 with fast track) from the 7th.

## Access arrangements

- For a reader, a scribe or extra time, the learner emails a request and evidence (a doctor's letter, a diagnosis letter or an EHCP) to [test@astarequivalency.co.uk](mailto:test@astarequivalency.co.uk).
- **25% extra time** can only be applied to the **10am and 2pm weekday slots**.
- We need at least **48 hours** to apply arrangements. Ideally they send the request and evidence on the day they buy.
