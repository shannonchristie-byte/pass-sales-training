Ready replies for learners asking about free or funded Functional Skills. Who qualifies and how it works is on [Funded courses](#/kb/p?slug=pfs-funded); the phone script is [Funded course calls](#/kb/p?slug=scripts-funded).

## Is it free?

> We don't have a totally free government-funded option, but we do have a funded route for Functional Skills where we may be able to cover the cost of the courses (currently £284 each). You'd still need to cover the cost of the exams (currently £215.20 per subject).
>
> We'd just need to ask you a few questions to see if you qualify. Payment for the exams has to be made before you can access the courses.
>
> The only way to get this qualification completely free is through a local college. That means attending in person every week for at least one full academic year, and you wouldn't get the qualification until at least next August, which most people can't do.
>
> With us everything is online, so you can study any time, anywhere, as quickly or slowly as you like. We run exams every day of the year.

## The funding questions

> 1. What's your current employment status: full time, part time or unemployed?
> 2. Do you claim any benefits, Universal Credit or other financial support?
> 3. Do you have any children, or anyone else you look after?
> 4. What do you need Functional Skills for, and what's your deadline?

## They qualify

> Thank you for answering. Based on this I'm happy to put you through for the funding. That means we cover the cost of the course, with 12 months' access to the premium version of the learning platform. The exams are the only part each learner pays for.
>
> The exams are currently on sale at £215.20 per subject on the flexible "book now, choose the date later" option, so you don't need to pick a date now. It's secured, ready for when you feel confident.

## They don't qualify

> Unfortunately, based on your current situation the system isn't qualifying you for the funding. It's usually for learners on a low income with dependants who claim financial support.
>
> We do have other options that could suit you better, such as our bundle, which includes a free resit that you don't get with the funding. I can also talk you through discounts and payment plans.
