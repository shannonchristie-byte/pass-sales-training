In Tawk you can type "/" then **course** or **exams** for the saved replies.

## The course

> The courses are all online with 24/7 access, and cover everything you need to pass. They include:
>
> - Revision video tutorials on every topic
> - Practice questions
> - Exam questions with model solutions and video explanations
> - Mock papers with video solutions
> - Progress tracking
>
> We typically recommend around 50 hours of study per subject before taking the exam, but some people spend a lot less time and others more. It really depends on you. Of learners who complete the course, 93% pass.

## How long the course takes

> We advise that the course typically takes 50 hours per subject. Some take less, others more: it depends on the knowledge you start with and how you use the course. Some learners finish in a few weeks, others a few months.
>
> You have full access to the course for 12 months, which is more than enough time to finish.

## The exams

> All exams are taken online from home. You just need a computer or laptop with a webcam and microphone. Once you've passed, you'll get a digital certificate. Results with our quickest exam board take 2 to 6 working days.
>
> The maths exam is 2 hours long, in two parts done back to back: 30 minutes without a calculator and 90 minutes with one.
>
> For English there are two exams. Reading and writing lasts 2 hours in total. Speaking, listening and communication lasts 1 hour and is sat on a separate day.

## Speaking, listening and communication (SLC)

> The SLC exam is 1 hour long. You'll give a short spoken presentation of 4 to 7 minutes. A few other learners will be on the call doing their presentations too, and you'll be asked to ask and answer questions at the end of each one.

Video to share: [SLC explained](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VCOVV449J7g)

## Exam boards

> We use two major exam boards, TQUK and Open Awards. Both are Ofqual regulated, so they're equivalent to a GCSE and guaranteed to be recognised.
>
> **TQUK (automated invigilation), our recommended board:** the exam board sends you the details to sign in to their website on exam day. There are a few checks first, then you complete your exam on screen. Results take 2 to 6 working days.
>
> **Open Awards (human invigilation):** on exam day you're sent a link to a Zoom call with an invigilator, who sets you up and stays throughout to make sure it's in exam conditions. Results take up to 16 working days.

> **To check:** the hint sheet names two boards and gives TQUK results as 2 to 6 working days. The PFS policy also offers City and Guilds and gives TQUK results as 1 to 6 working days. Confirm which figures to give on chat.

## Is it recognised?

> All the exams we provide are Ofqual regulated, the same standard as GCSEs. This is the highest level of regulation possible, so Functional Skills Level 2 is nationally recognised as equivalent to a GCSE grade 4/C and accepted by universities, apprenticeships and employers.

If they need to be sure for a particular course or job, see ["I need to check it's accepted"](#/kb/p?slug=objection-check-accepted).

## Functional Skills or GCSE?

> Compared with the GCSE, Functional Skills Level 2 is:
>
> - Cheaper
> - Easier to pass
> - Half the study time (50 hours per subject, compared with 100 for a GCSE)
> - Online exams sat from home at any time
> - Results in just 2 to 6 working days
>
> It's equivalent to a GCSE grade 4. For maths or English we'd always recommend Functional Skills over the GCSE.
