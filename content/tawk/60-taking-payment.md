For a learner who wants to pay on chat without a call. Calls convert better, so try [getting them onto the phone](#/kb/p?slug=tawk-to-phone) first.

In Tawk you can type "/" then **paymentlinkdetails**, **payment-link** or **payment-link-done** for the saved replies.

## 1. Ask for their details

> Please could you send me the following details:
>
> - Open exam date, or a specific date and time (if so, which)
> - First and last name
> - Email
> - Phone number
> - Postcode
> - House number
> - Date of birth

## 2. Create the order and send the link

Create the order, then make a link from the right site's magic link generator (listed on [Websites, links and tools](#/kb/p?slug=resources-links)) with the learner's email.

> Here's the link to complete the purchase on your side: [link]. Click "pay for order" when it opens. The link only lasts a short time, so please use it now.

Check WooCommerce for the payment.

> **To check:** the hint sheet says the link lasts 10 to 15 minutes; the results day plan says 30 minutes and 5 clicks (with one line saying 15). Which is right? The hint sheet's older version of this reply gives the learner a shared account sign-in: that is left out here, and the magic link replaces it.

## 3. Once they've paid

> Looks like the order has been placed!
>
> You'll get a copy of the terms and conditions by email, along with an order confirmation.
>
> Please also look out for an email titled "Course Logins" or "Account Logins" (it may go to your spam or junk folder). It has your details for the shop account, where you manage your booking and set exam dates, and for the learning platform, where the revision courses are.

## Payment plans

> - **Klarna or PayPal** split the payment into 3 equal interest-free payments, once a month over 3 months.
> - **Clearpay** splits the payment into 4 equal interest-free payments, every 2 weeks over 6 weeks.
> - **Payl8r** is a regulated finance provider. Details are here: https://payl8r.com/retailers/payment-detail?retailer=completet954ge70jote
