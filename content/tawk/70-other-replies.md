## Training providers and employers

When a chat is from an organisation rather than a learner, collect these details, then pass it on with "Send to B2B Team" on the Sales CRM lead (create the lead first). See [Leads, callbacks and the Sales CRM](#/kb/p?slug=rules-crm-leads).

> Please could you send me:
>
> - Your first and last name
> - Contact number
> - Email address
> - Organisation name
> - A preferred time for a call (if any)
> - Roughly how many learners
> - Courses, exams or both?

## Pass GCSE: the course

> The courses are all online with 24/7 access, and cover everything you need to pass. They include:
>
> - Revision materials on every topic
> - Practice questions
> - Exam questions with model solutions
> - A full collection of mock papers
> - Progress tracking
>
> We typically recommend around 100 hours of study per subject before the exam, but some people spend a lot less time and others more. It really depends on you.
