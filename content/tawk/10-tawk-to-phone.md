Tawk live chat is one of the best sources of interested learners. **Aim: get potential sales onto the phone as soon as possible**, because calls convert much better than chats.

1. Ask the standard questions: what they need the qualification for, and by when.
2. Reassure them that we help thousands of people in their situation.
3. **Give a reason for a call**, for example: "Our NHS support team can talk through the extra support available for you better than I can." Always give a reason: the phone team can offer specialist support the live chat team can't.
4. Try to get them to agree to a call now. If they can't, arrange a better time for a callback.
5. Add them to the callback list, with all the information in the right fields.
