Ready replies for the PFS questions that come up most on chat. For phone calls, see [objection handling](#/kb/s?key=objections).

## "Do I have to do the course?"

> There's no obligation to do the revision course. We just don't recommend sitting the exam without some kind of structured revision (a tutor, college course or online course). There's a lot to cover, as it's equivalent to a GCSE, and it's very difficult to pass otherwise.
>
> Our course is the natural choice for most learners, because it works out cheaper bought with the exam, and you get a free resit too.

See also [PFS exam without a course](#/kb/p?slug=scripts-pfs-no-course).

## "Do I get a tutor?"

> There's no direct access to a tutor, but the course does what a tutor would:
>
> - You can track and review your own progress with the progress tracking.
> - You'll know which areas to improve from the detailed feedback on every mock exam.
> - You'll know exactly when you're ready for the exam from the average scores on your dashboard.
>
> We can put you in touch with a tutor if you need one (at a separate cost), but we always recommend giving the course a good try first. Learners are often surprised how quickly they progress when they can review their own revision.

## "Which level should I do?"

> We have a free initial assessment on the free trial of our course: https://learn.passfunctionalskills.co.uk/register. It will tell you which level you're working at.
>
> That said, most learners book straight onto Level 2 whatever their score, as it's the level equivalent to a GCSE grade 4 (a pass). The Level 2 course is designed to be accessible for everyone and starts with very basic topics.

See also ["I'm not sure what level to start on"](#/kb/p?slug=objection-which-level).
